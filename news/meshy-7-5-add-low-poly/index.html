Meshy 7.5 makes high- and low-poly meshes in one run. Which should you use?

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

Vendor announcement Nov 27, 2026

Meshy 7.5 became available to all registered users on November 27, 2026. Turn on Add Low-Poly to get the original high-poly mesh and a reduced mesh with a baked normal map in the same run. The option adds 10 Meshy credits per generation.

Meshy launch: high- and low-poly meshes, settings and credits

Choose a setting for the camera distance

Consider an airship that appears more than once in a scene. Keep the high-poly mesh as a close-up reference, or start with More Faces when the outline needs to hold up near the camera. Try Fewer Faces for distant copies where the scene has a tighter polygon budget. Meshy’s airship comparison shows both settings side by side as shaded renders and wireframes.

  • Meshy launch: high- and low-poly meshes, settings and credits

Import the low-poly mesh with its normal map

The normal map carries surface detail into the lighting, while the mesh sets the silhouette. Check that the imported material uses the matching map. At the camera distance you expect to ship, inspect the outline, seams and small raised features. Review one representative prop in your target engine or on the target device before choosing a setting for the batch.

  • Meshy launch: high- and low-poly meshes, settings and credits

Budget the extra credits before generating a batch

The 10 credits for Add Low-Poly are added to the base cost of the generation. Approve one prop’s outline and material first, then estimate how many runs the remaining assets need. Meshy’s pricing page lists monthly credit amounts and download conditions by plan.
