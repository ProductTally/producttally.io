Raycast adds Dictation Mode on iOS. How do you draft a follow-up email?

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

Vendor announcement Nov 26, 2026

Raycast released iOS v1.5.6 on November 26, 2026, adding a dedicated Dictation Mode to its keyboard. With Raycast Pro dictation, you can open that layout, pick the Email style and use Review first to check the transcript before placing it in a draft.

Raycast release notes for iOS 1.5.6 · Raycast Manual: keyboard setup and Dictation Mode

Open the dedicated dictation layout

Install and enable the Raycast Keyboard. Keyboard dictation needs iOS Full Access. In Raycast’s keyboard settings, use Primary Mode, then open Custom Keys and turn on Open in Dictation Mode. Tap Show Keyboard whenever you want the full typing layout.

  • Raycast release notes for iOS 1.5.6
  • Raycast Manual: keyboard setup and Dictation Mode
  • Raycast pricing: Pro dictation

Dictate one short client follow-up

Under the keyboard’s Features settings, change Insert mode to Review first. Open an email draft, select the Email style, and speak a short follow-up with one next action. For example, ask the client to approve a draft by Tuesday. Finish recording and check the client name, project and date before inserting the text.

  • Raycast Manual: keyboard setup and Dictation Mode
  • Raycast Manual: Email style and Review first

Pick the language for this recording

If you need to dictate in another language, touch and hold the dictation key and choose from your available languages. Edit that list under Dictation Languages in the keyboard’s Features settings. The update also adds nine typing languages that you can download in the keyboard’s language settings.
