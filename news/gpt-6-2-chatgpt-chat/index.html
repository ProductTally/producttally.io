GPT‑6.2 comes to ChatGPT’s Chat tab: who gets it and what changes

By Evan · Published Nov 24, 2026 · Updated Nov 24, 2026

Vendor announcement Nov 23, 2026

On November 23, 2026, OpenAI began rolling out a version of GPT‑6.2 Sol tuned for the Chat tab to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users worldwide. Enterprise access depends on workspace admin settings. OpenAI reports gains in image understanding and in following complex instructions.

OpenAI release notes: GPT‑6.2 in Chat on November 23

Two GPT‑6.2 announcements in one week

On November 17, OpenAI introduced GPT‑6.2 Astra and Sol in ChatGPT Work and Codex. The November 23 announcement covers regular Chat, and the models powering Work and Codex stay as they were for this release. Free and Go are outside the announced Chat rollout. If you’re checking a managed Enterprise account, confirm your workspace settings as well as your plan.

What OpenAI measured in regular Chat

In OpenAI’s Chat tab evaluations, GPT‑6.2 scored 71.7% on image understanding versus 47.4% for GPT‑6. On especially challenging prompts, it followed instructions 78.1% of the time versus 62.5% for GPT‑6. These vendor results describe performance on the evaluated prompts.
