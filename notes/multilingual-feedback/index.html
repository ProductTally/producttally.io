A feedback form that speaks more than one language.

From bilingual fields to a CSV you can actually use.

EBy Evan ·2026-11-06
FORM / 01
✓
01Keep the first form small02Test the round trip03Look at the narrow version04Keep the next questions visible

Keep the first form small

We started with two fields: “Language / 语言” and “Feedback / 反馈”. Bilingual labels help readers understand the task. They are not the same as automatic interface translation.

Test the round trip

We submitted sample values containing Chinese, English, and Spanish, then checked the saved backend record. The downloaded CSV retained the bilingual headings and the submitted characters.

Look at the narrow version

At a 390px layout width, the title wrapped and the fields and submit button remained visible. This checks one layout, not input on every real mobile device.

Keep the next questions visible

Before collecting real feedback, test notification delivery, spam controls, your privacy wording, and any conditional logic you need. These were outside this first check.
