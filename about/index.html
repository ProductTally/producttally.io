About the project

A little less noise. A little more useful.

ProductTally is an independent notebook for people who create, write, build, and work in small teams.

Edited by Evan, this project brings together practical software observations and guides for an international readership. The focus is simple: help you understand a product before you make room for it in your workflow.

We describe what we actually checked, keep untested features visible, and link to official documentation so you can explore further.

The notebook starts small. A basic test is exactly that: a test of a particular task. It is not a guarantee about every feature, language, integration, or future release.
