How we review products

  • Start with a real task

    We start with a job you might do, such as adding captions, publishing a page, or organizing a small team’s work.

  • Show where the facts come from

    We cite official sources. When we test a workflow ourselves, we describe the steps and the scope of the test.

  • Explain what affects the decision

    We call out the plan, export options, language support, and other limits when they matter to the task.
