Quick answer

Try Silence Removal in Movavi for Windows if your talking-head footage has repeated gaps between takes. For interviews with meaningful pauses, use a copy and listen through every cut before adopting automation.

Movavi Video Editor Review

Choose the pauses you want to keep

Record roughly 90 seconds, with a half-second pause mid-sentence, a two-second gap between takes and a quiet word at the end of a sentence. Note the three places and duplicate the clip. Aim to shorten the long gap while keeping the short pause and the quiet word.

Compare automatic and conservative cuts

Run Auto on one copy. On the other, try a minimum pause longer than one second and adjust the volume threshold. If the tool removes a quiet word, lower the silence threshold and listen again. The figure shows generated speech with a gap shortened from two seconds to half a second outside Movavi.

Count the time spent correcting cuts

For each version, write down seconds removed, unwanted cuts and time spent restoring them. A single gap may be faster to cut by hand. Repeated long gaps with few repairs give automation a useful role.

Check the delivery format before buying

Make captions after the cut and check the words around joins. Movavi’s tutorial lists watermark, length, quality and codec limits for free exports. Give those limits your own delivery test. The product button opens the Windows page. ProductTally did not test Movavi’s detection or export.
