How to remove pauses in Movavi Video Editor without cutting words

Use a 90-second talking-head clip to compare automatic silence removal with a more conservative threshold.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Record a clip with three kinds of pause
  3. Compare automatic and manual settings
  4. Generate captions after the timing is approved

Quick answer

On Movavi Video Editor for Windows, use Silence Removal on a duplicate clip, then listen across each cut. Keep an unedited copy and check quiet words before generating captions. The sequence below is a suggested test for one clip.

Movavi Video Editor Review

Record a clip with three kinds of pause

Use 90 seconds of speech. Include a pause of about half a second mid-sentence, a two-second gap between takes and a quiet word near the end of a sentence. Note the three approximate timestamps. Duplicate the project or clip so the comparison starts from the same recording.

Compare automatic and manual settings

Select the clip and run Silence Removal in Auto. On the copy, use the manual controls for pause duration and volume threshold, starting with a longer minimum pause. Review transitions on headphones. If soft syllables disappear, lower the volume threshold and preview again. Record the total seconds removed and any cuts you restored.

Generate captions after the timing is approved

Keep the edit that removes the long gaps while preserving words and useful breaths. After finalizing the cuts, generate captions and check the first and last word around each join. Export a sample and watch it on a phone before applying the workflow to longer footage. The product link below opens Movavi’s Windows landing page.
