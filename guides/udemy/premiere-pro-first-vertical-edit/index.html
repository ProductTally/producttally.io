Which Udemy Premiere Pro course helps you finish a 30-second vertical edit?

Quick answer

Choose a Premiere Pro course by the first file you need to deliver. For a vertical demo, check whether the course teaches timeline editing, clear speech and H.264 export. The public outline for Premiere Pro for Beginners – Learn by Doing covers the first sequence, exercise files, trimming and H.264. Check vertical framing separately before enrolling.

Film one task and plan five shots

Download the shot list. For a desktop cable clip, film the tangled cable, the latch opening, the cable going into the clip, the finished desk and a three-second end frame. The five cuts are at 0, 5, 12, 21 and 27 seconds, with the ending at 30. Use the same camera angle for the opening and the finished desk so the difference is visible.

Make a 9:16 sequence and review the export

Create a 1080 × 1920 sequence. If the original is horizontal, try Auto Reframe, then check the latch and hand movement shot by shot. Export an H.264 MP4 and open it on a phone. At 00:12 you should see the cable clearly entering the clip, and at 00:27 the call to action should stay visible for three seconds. Listen for clean speech and check captions against the destination app’s interface. These are proposed acceptance checks.

Watch the course preview before you pay

Premiere Pro for Beginners – Learn by Doing lists exercise files, a 10-minute first-sequence preview and an H.264 export preview. Check the previews against your own Premiere version. We did not confirm that the public syllabus walks through a 9:16 delivery with caption placement. If sequence setup and export are already familiar, Adobe’s reframing and caption guides may cover what you need. This assessment uses the public outline and our practice brief.
