Udemy Black Friday 2026: how to choose an AI workflow course

Use an interview-to-review project to compare AI course outlines, sample files and the final price.

Quick answer

Pick a project before comparing Udemy’s Black Friday course offers. For a small content team, look for a course that turns an interview transcript into a cited list of action items that a person can approve. Check that the public outline includes an end-to-end assignment and verify the total in your own account.

Udemy Review

Write down the output you need

Try a fictional six-line transcript. Include one task with an explicit owner and date, a second without an owner and an unrelated aside. The exercise should return only two action items, cite the supporting lines and flag the missing owner for review. Keep customer or employee data out of the exercise.

Sample transcript: (1) The episode is about studio lighting. (2) Maya will draft three interview questions by Friday. (3) The venue has a coffee cart. (4) Someone needs to confirm the release date. (5) No owner was assigned for that check. (6) The recording lasted 20 minutes.

Inspect the outline and a preview

Look for downloadable inputs, structured extraction, a manual review step and an example where the workflow fails or the input is incomplete. Check which tool version the instructor uses, whether you need an API account and whether there are separate usage costs. Check if you can reproduce the assignment on your current plan.

Check the cost before enrolling

Open the official Black Friday page, shortlist one course and compare the price shown in your own checkout with your real alternatives. If you have Personal Plan, check whether the exact course is included. Prefer buying when you have identified a project you can complete this week. We reviewed public material and proposed the exercise. We did not enroll or measure course results.
