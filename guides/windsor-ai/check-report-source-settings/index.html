How to reconcile a GA4 report before syncing it with Windsor.ai

Compare one closed reporting window with the original property and find a missing date before scheduling a sync.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Choose a small comparison you can repeat
  3. Match coverage before comparing totals
  4. Confirm the sheet preserves a verified preview

Quick answer

Before scheduling a Windsor.ai sync, compare Date and Sessions from one GA4 property against the same closed window and property time zone. In our November 11 check we reached Add data without authorizing a connector. The reconciliation examples are illustrative.

Windsor.ai Review

Choose a small comparison you can repeat

Use one GA4 property, a fixed three-day window that excludes today, and Date plus Sessions only. Record the property time zone and any segment or filter. Compare with the original source under the same conditions. Add channel, country or campaign only after the daily totals match.

Match coverage before comparing totals

In a fictional baseline the three days are 18, 22 and 12 sessions, totaling 52. If your preview shows only 18 and 22, the total is 40. Start by finding the missing date. Check the inclusive dates, property and filters before treating the gap as traffic loss. If the date is present with zero, keep that distinct from a missing row and check the source.

Confirm the sheet preserves a verified preview

Send the small result to a scratch sheet and check that all three dates, the header and the Sessions column match. If preview agrees but Sheets differs, inspect date parsing, column order and existing sheet filters or formulas. Schedule the recurring feed only after the source, preview and sheet agree. Our account check did not verify syncing or reported numbers.
