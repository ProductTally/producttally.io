What is Nifty for? Managing launches with multiple owners

See where Nifty fits when a team needs project briefs, task dependencies and milestones together.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Keep the brief close to the launch work
  3. Decide whether your team needs milestones
  4. Check how the project will be shared

Quick answer

Nifty is a project workspace for teams coordinating work across several people and stages. It makes the most sense when one missed handoff can delay a launch.

Nifty Review

Keep the brief close to the launch work

For a newsletter redesign, the writer, designer and reviewer can work from the same project brief and track their tasks in the same place. A milestone can show whether the project is ready for editorial review.

Decide whether your team needs milestones

A roadmap helps when the next owner needs to see which prerequisite is blocking the work. A Capterra reviewer who shared progress with clients found that milestones took time to learn. A solo creator who tracks only a due date may get little from the extra setup.

Check how the project will be shared

Before standardizing on Nifty, look at the roles, reporting and export your team needs. The related launch guide covers our single-user trial of dependencies and exports. Client access calls for a separate check.
