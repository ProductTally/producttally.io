AI SEO for small websites: a practical GEO checklist

Use established SEO basics and verified evidence to improve AI search visibility on a small site. Learn what to measure and which GEO shortcuts to skip.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

Quick answer

For Google’s generative search features, keep the same SEO foundations: make useful original pages crawlable and eligible for Search, explain claims with evidence, and keep technical details accurate. Google says no special GEO markup or ideal page length is required, and indexing or inclusion is never guaranteed.

Make the page findable and understandable

Check that the URL returns a successful response, is not blocked from crawling, has a self-consistent canonical URL, and appears in the sitemap. Use descriptive titles, one clear main heading, useful internal links, and visible page text that does not depend on a blocked script. Eligibility does not guarantee indexing.

Add information that a generic summary cannot supply

Include first-hand testing, the method, date, audience, limitations, and links to primary sources. Separate what the organization states from what you observed. Update pricing, features, and policies with a checked date; do not leave time-sensitive claims looking permanent.

Answer the decision question, then give the reasoning

State a concise answer near the beginning, then explain steps, trade-offs, and exceptions in descriptive sections. Use examples only when they are real and reproducible. There is no requirement to split every thought into tiny chunks or repeat every keyword variant.

Use structured data carefully and track visibility

Keep structured data consistent with text users can see; it can support eligible search features but is not a special GEO pass. Google’s Search Console generative AI report shows impressions and pages for AI Overviews and AI Mode. Treat third-party visibility scores as estimates, not access to a search engine’s internal ranking data.

Map one reader intent to one useful page

Build a question set from real reader tasks: learning, comparing, choosing, and using a product. Assign each main intent one canonical guide instead of publishing near-duplicate pages for every wording. Lead with an answer, then show evidence, method, and limits. Link onward only to the review or product page that actually fits the question.

Measure citations, brand mentions, and outcomes separately

Bing AI Performance reports cited pages and grouped grounding queries, but citation counts are not rankings or traffic. Google’s AI report measures impressions, while clicks and visits must be read in the other available reports and analytics. For ChatGPT Search, check OAI-SearchBot access; it is separate from GPTBot training controls. Recheck the same questions, locale, and region across platforms, and track citations, brand mentions, referrals, and signups as separate measures.
