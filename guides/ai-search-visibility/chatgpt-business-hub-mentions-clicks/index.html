ChatGPT Business Hub: how to claim your business and read mentions and clicks

Learn who can claim a business in ChatGPT, what the 28-day Insights report counts, and how to use it to check a SaaS page.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Check claim and agent eligibility separately
  3. Read Insights over a comparable 28-day window
  4. Turn a theme into one change on a pricing page
  5. Review the change without overstating attribution

Quick answer

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on November 10, 2026. Business Hub lets eligible businesses verify their profile and see 28 days of aggregate activity. For online businesses, a mention means ChatGPT referenced the website; a website click records someone following a link. Neither count establishes that ChatGPT recommended the product.

Check claim and agent eligibility separately

Search for your business in Business Hub. If the listing matches and you are authorized to represent it, choose one of the verification methods offered. After claim approval, OpenAI requires Persona identity verification to view Insights or publish profile edits. Availability depends on business type, location and supported verification. Business Agents initially serve eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses with a website; their conversations are labeled separately from independent ChatGPT answers.

Read Insights over a comparable 28-day window

For an online business, a brand name by itself does not count as a mention. A local business mention counts when a location result is shown. Use website clicks as a count of click-throughs, and use your own analytics to study visits and signups. Discovery topics summarize themes, without exposing individual prompts. If a field is blank, mark it unavailable rather than entering zero.

Turn a theme into one change on a pricing page

Consider a small SaaS with a theme about client access. Ask whether a three-person team can invite a client to review a project without paying for another seat. Save the question, locale, date, cited URL and exact wording. Record whether the answer named the brand, cited the page or recommended the plan for this use case. If the pricing page leaves guest billing unclear, verify the rule and add it next to the plan.

Review the change without overstating attribution

Note the edit date and repeat the same question under comparable conditions. Compare complete, non-overlapping 28-day Business Hub windows when data is available. Record mentions, clicks and site signups separately. The answer sample shows whether one response used the right facts; changes in aggregate activity can have multiple causes.
