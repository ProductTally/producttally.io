How to track AI traffic in GA4 with the AI Assistant channel

Find visits referred by AI assistants, inspect the pages they land on, and compare useful actions without mistaking referral sessions for citations or all AI search exposure.

By Evan · Updated Nov 10, 2026

On this page

  3. Open the session report and isolate the channel
  4. Inspect sources, landing pages, and useful actions

Quick answer

In GA4, open Reports > Acquisition > Traffic acquisition and filter the session default channel group to AI Assistant. Change the table dimension to Session source / medium and add landing page and engagement or key-event metrics. This channel tracks identifiable visits from recognized assistants; Google classifies clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode under Organic Search, and measures their visibility separately in Search Console.

What GA4’s AI Assistant channel counts

Google added AI Assistant traffic measurement to GA4 on May 13, 2026. When a session referrer matches a recognized assistant, GA4 can assign the `ai-assistant` medium and group that visit under AI Assistant. Google lists ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Copilot, and Grok as examples. Its current channel definition explicitly excludes Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, which are included in Organic Search. The recognized-source rules may evolve, so check the current definition when reviewing a future report.

Open the session report and isolate the channel

Select the correct GA4 property and date range, then open Reports > Acquisition > Traffic acquisition. The report’s default dimension is Session default channel grouping. Find AI Assistant in the table or use the report filter to include that channel. If the Acquisition report is missing from the left menu, an editor may need to restore it to the report collection. Use a complete month and compare it with the preceding month only when both periods have enough activity to interpret.

Inspect sources, landing pages, and useful actions

Switch the table dimension to Session source / medium to see which recognized assistant sent the visit. Add or filter by landing page so you can tell whether people arrived on a guide, product page, or another URL. Review sessions beside engaged sessions, engagement rate, average engagement time per session, and key events that you have actually configured, such as completed signup or trial start. Keep counts next to rates: a small number of visits can make percentage changes look dramatic.

Use session scope when you mean a visit

Traffic acquisition is session-scoped: it attributes the current visit to its session source. User acquisition is user-scoped and reports the channel that first brought a person to the site. Those views answer different questions and their engagement values should not be compared as if they use the same denominator. For “What did AI-referred visits do this month?”, use session dimensions in Traffic acquisition.

Keep AI referrals separate from AI search visibility

GA4 can show a visit that arrived from a link in an assistant, but it cannot show an answer citation that generated no click or reliably reveal the prompt that led to the visit. For Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, use the Generative AI performance report in Search Console; Google reports impressions and the pages shown, while GA4 records subsequent visits and configured events. Bing AI Performance reports citations and grounding queries. These are different signals; do not add them together into one “AI traffic” total.

Diagnose missing or misclassified visits

If AI Assistant is empty, first check the property, date range, report dimension, and whether the site has recorded sessions. Then inspect Session source / medium for identifiable assistant referrers. A visit whose referrer information was lost can appear as direct / none; Google also documents redirects, missing campaign information, and browser blocking as reasons traffic source details can be absent. Treat the channel as a measurable floor, not a complete count of people influenced by AI.

Make a small monthly review you can repeat

Save the date range, AI Assistant sessions, sources, landing pages, engaged sessions, and configured key events. Put Search Console AI impressions and Bing citations in separate columns or reports, and note any page edits beside the period they affect. Compare the same date windows and site sections each month. Use the report to choose a page for closer review; it does not establish that an AI mention caused a signup or that a copy change caused a later citation.
