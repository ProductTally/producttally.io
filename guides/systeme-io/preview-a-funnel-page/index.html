Systeme.io guide: build a landing page and plan your first email funnel

Start with a draft, check the mobile layout and map the path from a signup form to a welcome email.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Create a draft before inviting visitors
  3. Review the narrow layout before launch
  4. Follow a visitor from signup to the welcome email

Quick answer

Systeme.io fits creators who want a landing page, email and simple automation in one place. A free lead magnet makes a practical first funnel because you can judge the signup and delivery without introducing a payment step. On November 11, 2026, an information page in an inactive free funnel retained English, Chinese and Japanese headings after saving and reloading. The editor offered a mobile preview.

systeme.io Review

Create a draft before inviting visitors

Create a custom funnel and leave it inactive while editing. Add an information step, choose a template and replace its heading with your actual offer in the audience’s language.

Review the narrow layout before launch

Save, reload and verify the text. Use mobile preview to check reading order and wrapping. Before a real launch, check the live page and any forms on a phone. Sending email and collecting payments require their own setup and checks.

Follow a visitor from signup to the welcome email

When you are ready for a private test, add an opt-in step and a thank-you step. Use the rule in the official help article to send a welcome email when someone submits the form. Use a test address you control. Check the confirmation screen on a phone, that the contact is saved, and that the email arrives with the correct link. Our walkthrough covered the page editor and mobile preview; it did not verify email delivery or payments.

An incentivized G2 review liked having signup, email and course access in one workflow, while finding templates and funnel analysis limited. If you need a custom design or detailed reporting at each stage, compare those requirements against the editor and reports before migrating a live campaign.
