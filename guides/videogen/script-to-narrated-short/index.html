VideoGen script to video: budget a 30-second narrated short

Plan a vertical demo, estimate the cost of replacing a clip, and check the MP4 before posting.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Storyboard a 30-second demo with footage you own
  3. Set a budget for retakes before generating
  4. Review the downloaded file on a phone

Quick answer

For a short narrated explainer, use VideoGen’s Script to video workflow and check the generated footage separately from export. As of November 16, 2026, the rate card lists 20 credits per generated second of Standard AI video, zero export credits at 1080p, and two per output second at 4K. This guide uses official documentation and a worked example.

VideoGen Review

Storyboard a 30-second demo with footage you own

Try three beats for a cable organizer: show the tangled desk, fasten the cables, then show the cleared surface. Film the product yourself if the fit or mechanism matters. Save AI visuals for generic cutaways. In Script to video, paste the three short paragraphs, select vertical format and preview the narrator and captions.

Set a budget for retakes before generating

Say the cut uses a 10-second AI clip and you generate a second 10-second take at Standard. The AI-video charge for those two generations is 20 seconds × 20 = 400 credits, even if just one take makes the final cut. A 30-second 1080p export adds zero export credits. A 30-second 4K export adds 60 if available on your plan. This example excludes other credit-consuming steps.

Review the downloaded file on a phone

Start at 1080p for vertical social video. Check that the product name is pronounced correctly, captions avoid the app interface, and the final shot shows the claimed result. If the product visibly changes between cuts, replace the affected shot with your own footage. Check the account’s plan and remaining credits before generating more. No paid render or export was performed for this guide.
