What is Mangools for? Keyword research for smaller sites

See where KWFinder and the wider Mangools suite fit when you need to turn search data into a content decision.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Start with a question your site can answer
  3. Use scores to narrow the list
  4. Size the plan to your research workload

Quick answer

Mangools is an SEO suite for focused research. Its KWFinder tool suits publishers and small teams that want keyword ideas, a view of the search results and a shortlist for their next article.

Mangools Review

Start with a question your site can answer

A small creator site choosing between a broad term and a specific tutorial can compare the actual pages shown for both searches. A list of templates and a page of tutorials point to different reader needs, even when both queries sound similar.

Use scores to narrow the list

KWFinder exposes metrics alongside search results. Treat estimated volume and difficulty as signals, then decide where your article can add something useful. A G2 reviewer used Mangools for keyword research and rank tracking and wanted easier bulk difficulty checks.

Size the plan to your research workload

For a few articles, a shortlist and CSV may be enough. If you routinely evaluate many markets, count lookups and check what the team needs beyond KWFinder. The related guide records the shortlist and export that we tried.
