Pombo onboarding: test which reminder stops after activation

Use two test contacts and one activation event to check who should get the day-one reminder.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Define two events and one reminder
  3. Confirm the timeline before you send
  4. Find out why an activated contact stayed eligible

Quick answer

In Pombo, start a welcome sequence on signup and set first_report_created as the goal with stopping enabled. In the example below, both contacts can receive the welcome email. Only the contact who has not created a report should remain eligible for the next reminder.

Download the two-contact acceptance checklist

Pombo Review

Define two events and one reminder

For a sample reporting app, enroll A and B with user.created at 09:00 UTC on day 0. Send first_report_created for A at 13:00 UTC from the app after the first report is saved. Use the same contact identity that enrolled A. B gets no activation event. Schedule the reminder 24 hours after the welcome step.

Confirm the timeline before you send

In draft mode, use Simulate to inspect the email content, matching audience and branch. At day 1, 09:00 UTC, A should be stopped and B eligible. Record the actual result in the CSV. Simulation does not send mail or enroll contacts. To confirm your integration, record the app event and Pombo outcome with two controlled, consenting test contacts after the sending requirements are met.

Find out why an activated contact stayed eligible

If A is still eligible, check the event name, contact identity, event time and the stop-on-goal setting before changing the email. If neither contact can send, check approval, sending-domain authentication and send limits separately. This guide describes a proposed test; no campaign was activated.
