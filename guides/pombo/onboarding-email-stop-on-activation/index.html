Pombo onboarding emails: stop reminders when users activate

Set a signup trigger and an activation goal in Pombo. Verify two contact paths before turning on a SaaS welcome sequence.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Choose an event that shows real progress
  3. Compare two paths before sending
  4. Know what still needs verification

Quick answer

In Pombo, start a welcome sequence on a signup event and set a second event as its goal. Enable stopping on goal completion so users who already activated skip the remaining reminders. Use simulation to inspect what a contact would receive before enabling sends.

Pombo Review

Choose an event that shows real progress

For a hypothetical reporting app, use `user.created` for signup and `first_report_created` for activation. Use the same contact email on both events. Keep the first reminder focused on one action, such as creating a sample report. A signup or an email open alone does not demonstrate that the report exists.

Compare two paths before sending

Use test contacts you control. Both should receive the welcome step in your example. Contact A reaches the goal before the first scheduled reminder and should skip the remaining steps with goal stopping enabled. Contact B does not reach the goal and should remain eligible. Use simulation to inspect matching, timing and rendered links. Use a controlled end-to-end event check if the simulation cannot establish that your app delivered the goal event at the intended point.

Know what still needs verification

Simulation sends no email and creates no enrollment. Before a live run, verify account approval, recipient permission, sending-domain authentication and the account’s send allowance. For a controlled first cohort, confirm that reminder eligibility and the reported goal match the events you recorded.
