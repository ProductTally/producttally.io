How to check quso.ai credits before clipping or captioning a video

Import a short sample, read the estimate and compare your balance with the plans or one-time credit packs your account shows.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Choose a workflow and import a sample
  3. Read the estimate alongside your balance
  4. Compare what your account actually offers

Quick answer

On November 11, 2026, both quso.ai import flows accepted our 63.17-second synthetic MP4 and estimated about 2 credits. The English Pay as you go workspace had 0 credits, so the two generation buttons were disabled.

quso.ai Review

Choose a workflow and import a sample

Open Long to shorts to prepare a clipping job or AI Captions to set up subtitles. Choose Local files and select a short MP4 you can use for testing. Both flows displayed the same sample with a duration of 01:03.

Read the estimate alongside your balance

Check the estimate in the setup screen. This sample was rounded to about two minutes and 2 credits in both flows. With 0 credits, Generate clips and Generate captions stayed disabled. The test ended at that screen. Clip selection, caption accuracy, output quality, export and watermarks were outside its scope.

Compare what your account actually offers

Open Buy credits and note the currency and whether the price is for a pack or a subscription. Our account offered one-time packs of 100 credits for HK$19, 250 for HK$38 and 500 for HK$66. We made no purchase. The official FAQ says the 75-credit monthly free plan is available in some countries. Use your own account’s offer to budget a trial.
