How to publish your first Castos podcast episode from WordPress

Choose a publishing editor and a feed, then verify an episode page, an RSS entry and its audio before submitting to directories.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Decide where the editor will make changes
  3. Keep a single RSS address for your listeners
  4. Check the page, the feed item and the audio
  5. Review what still depends on the account and plan
  6. Choose where the episode is edited before moving a show

Quick answer

If WordPress owns the episode page, publish through Seriously Simple Podcasting and use Castos to host the audio. Choose one feed to give to directories. Before submitting it, check that the episode title, RSS enclosure and audio playback all belong to the same episode.

Castos Review

Decide where the editor will make changes

Prepare a show title, square cover and a short trailer MP3. In WordPress, follow the Seriously Simple Podcasting quickstart to connect the intended Castos show. Manage the episode in WordPress so the text and audio can sync to Castos. The vendor explains that direct Castos edits do not push back into WordPress. If your team wants to edit in Castos, use its dashboard workflow and embed the player on WordPress.

Keep a single RSS address for your listeners

The plugin and Castos can each provide a feed. Decide which to publish in directories and verify it after changing hosting or distribution settings. Castos recommends its hosted feed for most new shows. For an existing show, record the current feed and redirect plan before switching. Use a public trailer only when you want it visible. A private show’s feed belongs with invited listeners, not public directories.

Check the page, the feed item and the audio

Open the episode while signed out. Compare its title with the matching item in the chosen RSS, then check its GUID and the HTTPS enclosure URL, type and length. Play the episode and seek forward. On November 10 we did these read-only checks against Castos’s public Audience example. Its item had an audio/mpeg enclosure and the public player played and sought. The feed snapshot had a duplicate GUID elsewhere, so check distinct IDs on your own feed and repeat after adding a second episode.

Review what still depends on the account and plan

Submitting the public feed to a directory is separate from publishing a page. Decide who will own that submission. The public sample does not establish what happens when you upload your own episode or change a private listener’s access. Our creator-account setup stopped at the trial card screen. Check the current plan and payment terms before starting a trial.

Choose where the episode is edited before moving a show

Castos documents two publishing routes: its dashboard or WordPress with Seriously Simple Podcasting. A G2 user liked publishing in WordPress and having Castos sync the episode. Another wished their prior analytics carried over during a migration. Before switching hosts, decide which editor owns the episode, save a copy of your existing analytics and confirm how the feed redirect will be handled. For a private show, check that an invited listener can subscribe with their own feed.
