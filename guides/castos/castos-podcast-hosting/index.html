What is Castos for? Podcast hosting for WordPress and private shows

Find where Castos fits between an episode website, audio hosting and public or private RSS.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Choose where editors publish
  3. Think about the listener and the feed
  4. Choose based on ownership and publishing needs

Quick answer

Castos hosts podcast audio and distributes it through RSS. It is worth considering for WordPress publishers who want episodes beside their other content, or teams that need a private feed.

Castos Review

Choose where editors publish

Castos documents two options: manage episodes in the Castos dashboard, or publish from WordPress with Seriously Simple Podcasting. The dashboard gives a team a dedicated place to work. WordPress fits a publisher that already manages show notes, articles and episode pages there.

Think about the listener and the feed

Public feeds suit a show meant to be discovered. For private audio, decide how listeners get access and whether you need to revoke individual access later. For a migration, check who controls the current feed, how the redirect will work and which analytics can be retained. A G2 reviewer called out the last point.

Choose based on ownership and publishing needs

A single public show can start with a simpler hosting setup. WordPress publishing or private access gives you more reason to evaluate Castos. The related podcast setup guide uses official documentation and community reports.
