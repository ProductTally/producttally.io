Cal.com guest time zones: verify your booking link before sharing

Check one appointment in two time zones, then compare the final invitation when testing a live booking.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Choose one time that you can trace
  3. Check the invitation separately
  4. Fix availability at the source

Quick answer

In our November 11, 2026 Cal.com Individual account check, a 30-minute event on November 12 showed 09:00 in Shanghai and 10:00 in Tokyo. The Tokyo booking form kept the 10:00–10:30 slot. This verified the visitor time display through the form. Confirmation email and calendar conflicts were outside that test.

Cal.com Review

Choose one time that you can trace

Use an existing 30-minute event and record the host date, time and availability schedule. Open the booking link in a signed-out window. In the observed case, Shanghai showed 09:00–09:30 and Tokyo showed 10:00–10:30 on November 12. Both started at 01:00 UTC. Check the date again if the conversion crosses midnight.

Check the invitation separately

If you need to confirm delivery before sending the link to clients, use a personal test address and complete a test booking. Check the guest confirmation, host calendar and calendar invitation for the same instant and duration. Calendar apps may show different local clock times. Around daylight saving changes, repeat on a date on each side of the change. This is a suggested check.

Fix availability at the source

If the guest sees times you do not want to offer, check which availability schedule the event uses and its time zone. If your working hours need to follow the local clock while traveling, Cal.com documents that its travel schedule applies to default availability, while events using another schedule keep that schedule. Check your public page again after changing it.
