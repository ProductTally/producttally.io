What is Oh Dear for? Website monitoring for small teams

Compare page availability, broken-link checks and application monitoring before choosing what to monitor.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Monitor the pages visitors rely on
  3. Match the check to what could break
  4. Choose according to the sites you own

Quick answer

Oh Dear fits teams responsible for public websites that want to see availability, certificate and link problems together. It is particularly relevant when a homepage can work while an important article or download is broken.

Oh Dear Review

Monitor the pages visitors rely on

A publisher can group the homepage, key resources and a download path into a monitoring plan. The vendor lists uptime, certificates and link crawling. Together these checks cover common problems after a redesign or migration.

Match the check to what could break

An HTTP response can tell you a page is reachable. For a page returning an incorrect message with a successful status, the vendor documents a response-text check. Login and checkout require a separate check of the full flow. A Laravel community discussion also distinguished public availability checks from deeper application diagnostics.

Choose according to the sites you own

Count the sites and endpoints you need to monitor and identify who will respond to alerts. The related 404 article documents our controlled check and what the dashboard showed after recovery.
