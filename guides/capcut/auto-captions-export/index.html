CapCut web guide: generate, correct and export auto captions

Add subtitles to a short video in CapCut

CapCut · Open the web editor

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  3. Generate the first caption track

Quick answer

In our web test, a free account generated editable captions. Downloading the video with those captions required Pro.

CapCut Review

Import your clip

Bring one short clip with clear speech. Create a project, choose Upload → Upload file, then drag the clip onto the timeline. Press Play to check that you can hear the voice.

Generate the first caption track

Open Captions → Auto captions. Choose the language actually spoken in the clip and click Generate. A row of caption blocks above the video means the track is ready.

Fix words and timing

Select a block and edit the text in the captions panel. Check names, numbers and times against the audio. Play the clip from the start and check that each line appears as the speaker says it. For vertical clips, keep text clear of the bottom edge.

Open Captions → Auto captions, choose the spoken language and select Generate. On 11 November 2026 our new, unpaid web account generated four English (US) segments from a nine-second synthetic clip. It wrote “product tally”; we corrected it to “ProductTally” and confirmed the edit after reopening and updating the project. Review names, times and the split between lines.

Check the video download

Open Export → Download early. If a settings panel opens, choose a resolution and download a short preview to check the timing and readability. If the panel lists Auto captions as a Pro feature, use the next step to try an alternative.

Try manual captions or an SRT file

Create a separate short project. In Captions choose Manual captions to enter a few lines, or Upload caption file to import a UTF-8 .srt file. Check that editable blocks appear on the timeline. Try Export → Download again before editing the full video. We verified the automatic workflow and its Pro prompt; the download result for these alternatives is still unverified.
