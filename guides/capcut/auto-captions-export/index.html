CapCut web guide: generate, correct and export auto captions

Add captions to a short video and check whether your account can download the finished clip.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Import your clip
  3. Generate and correct captions
  4. Check download eligibility

Quick answer

In our web test, a free account generated editable captions. Downloading the video with those captions required Pro.

CapCut Review

Import your clip

Open Video Editor, create a project, then choose Upload → Upload file. Drag the clip from Media onto the timeline. Use a short clip you own with clear speech.

Generate and correct captions

Open Captions → Auto captions, choose the spoken language and select Generate. On 11 November 2026 our new, unpaid web account generated four English (US) segments from a nine-second synthetic clip. It wrote “product tally”; we corrected it to “ProductTally” and confirmed the edit after reopening and updating the project. Review names, times and the split between lines.

Check download eligibility

Open Export → Download before styling the whole clip. Our project showed “1 Pro benefit in use” for Auto captions and offered an upgrade. We obtained no video file. For a zero-budget project, try a separate short project with Manual captions and verify the actual download before committing to that workflow.
