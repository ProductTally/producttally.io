JoggAI URL to Video: turn a product page into a 15-second draft

Use one product page to plan three shots, review the imported claims and approve a short video draft.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Give the draft one job
  3. Review the imported material before rendering
  4. Approve it on a phone

Quick answer

JoggAI’s URL to Video workflow can use a product page to start a video draft. Use a page you control, confirm which assets and claims were imported, then cut the result to a 15-second target. This example is a review workflow, not a measured generation result.

JoggAI Review

Give the draft one job

Use a fictional 500 mL bottle. Plan 0–3 seconds to show the bottle, 3–11 to show its lid and capacity, and 11–15 for one action. Write down the confirmed specifications and allowed assets. Use a stable product name and exclude unsupported claims about insulation or leak resistance.

Review the imported material before rendering

Paste the product URL and inspect the draft’s images and script. Replace irrelevant photos and delete any invented claims. First revise the script for accuracy, then revise the visuals for framing. Check duration in the exported file and trim it if needed. The 15 seconds is your editing target.

Approve it on a phone

Check the opening frame, product close-up and final frame. Compare spoken and on-screen details with the source, especially capacity, price and variants. Confirm permission for the footage, music and voice. If one fact is wrong, fix that item before producing variants.
