JoggAI URL to Video: turn a product page into a 15-second draft

Start with one product variant, check the extracted claims and build a vertical ad draft around footage you can verify.

Quick answer

JoggAI URL to Video is useful for a first social ad draft when the product page has clear specs and usable photographs. It can gather visuals and draft narration from the link. Check the exact variant first, then review the script and media before rendering. Treat 15 seconds as your editing target and trim if the available duration is different.

JoggAI Review

Start with a single variant and one selling point

For this fictional product page, the approved details are a teal 500 mL bottle and a screw cap. The page has a full-product photo, a close-up of the cap and a separate photo of a red 750 mL variant. The draft should show only the teal model. Pick the 500 mL capacity and cap as the story; leave out claims about insulation or leak resistance unless you have separate evidence.

Check what JoggAI imported before generating

Open AI Video Ads, paste the URL and review the product information. If a preview draft says 750 mL or shows the red model, replace both with the approved assets and facts before making more variants. If the URL import cannot isolate the right variant, the feature page provides a manual product entry option. JoggAI documents avatar, voice, ratio and language controls, as well as the option to supply your own script.

Storyboard 15 seconds for a vertical ad

0–3 s: show the teal bottle in full so the viewer knows what is for sale. 3–11 s: use the cap close-up and an on-screen 500 mL label. 11–15 s: return to a clean product image and show an invitation to see the teal version. Keep the bottle in the vertical safe area and leave room for captions. The storyboard illustrates the intended edit.

Approve one actual export before making variants

Choose a vertical format for this example. Watch the export on a phone with the sound off, then listen for the name, capacity and any unapproved promise. Check the colour and cap against the product page, verify usable rights for media and voice, then open the landing page that the ad will link to. If a price could change, leave it out of the video. The workflow and storyboard are illustrative; no JoggAI render was measured.
