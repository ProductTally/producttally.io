How to publish a one-page portfolio with Gamma Sites

Build a one-page portfolio in Gamma, choose a domain and verify the published page title and description.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Turn your work into a page people can scan
  3. Choose the domain before sharing widely
  4. Check the title and discoverability after publishing

Quick answer

Use Gamma Sites for a compact portfolio with a few projects and one contact action. Publish on a gamma.site subdomain to review it quickly. If you need your own domain, check the plan and connect it using the DNS values Gamma supplies. Then verify the public page and its metadata.

Gamma Review

Turn your work into a page people can scan

Start with a clear role and audience, two projects, and a contact link. For each project, describe the problem and your contribution next to one piece of evidence you can share. Use the published preview to check the reading order and click through each project and contact link on a phone.

Choose the domain before sharing widely

Use the gamma.site address for an initial review. If the portfolio will be linked from proposals or a résumé, decide whether to publish on a domain you control before distributing it. Gamma documents custom domains for Pro and above. Use the records shown for your site, publish again and verify the final HTTPS address in a signed-out browser.

Check the title and discoverability after publishing

Set a specific page title and description in Gamma and publish the changes. Inspect the public page rather than relying on the editor. Confirm the title names your role and the description identifies your kind of work. If search discovery matters, review the indexing setting and check the public URL. Publishing and enabling indexing are steps you control, while inclusion in search results is a separate outcome.
