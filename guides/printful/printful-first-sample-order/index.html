Quick answer

Printful is worth sampling if you have artwork and a sales channel and want to try one T-shirt design without stocking a batch. You set the price and run the storefront. Printful prints, packs and ships after an order. Approve the physical sample and the regular order cost before listing.

Printful Review

When Printful fits your launch

Imagine selling one cream T-shirt with your podcast logo. Printful lets you keep your storefront and retail price while outsourcing production and dispatch. If you already sell at predictable volume or need a particular fabric or finish, request a bulk quote from a local printer and compare delivered unit costs and lead times.

Order the design you actually plan to sell

In Design Maker, save the model, color, size and print placement. Keep the small lettering and thin lines in your artwork so the sample can reveal problems. Printful’s sample-order help describes a 20% discount for eligible connected stores, generally from the following month. Check Billing → Discounts. Under New order, choose Test your design when it appears, or check the regular manual order quote.

Make three checks before listing

Print: check readability and compare placement with the mockup in daylight. Fit: measure width across the chest and length on the flat garment using the same method as the size chart. Wash: follow the care label once, then check for fading, peeling or noticeable shrinkage. Change the design or variant and resample if needed. Our illustration is a suggested inspection setup. ProductTally did not order a sample.

Calculate what one regular order leaves you

Suppose the buyer pays $28 and you cover $5 shipping. Deduct $13 production and fulfillment and $1.20 storefront and payment fees. That leaves $8.80 before returns, taxes or advertising. Replace the example with regular quotes for the same variant and destination. Keep the sample discount in the testing budget.

Preview one product and check the quote

Upload artwork you have permission to use in Design Maker. Preview the variant you intend to sell and check the shipping quote before committing to a sample.
