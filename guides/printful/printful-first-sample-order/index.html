How to order a Printful sample before listing your first T-shirt

Check one real sample for print quality, fit and shipping, then price the shirt using normal order costs.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Save the exact variant you plan to sell
  3. Check checkout and delivery separately
  4. Use regular costs for the retail price

Quick answer

Use one design, one shirt variant and a destination in your first sales market. Printful documents a 20% sample discount for eligible accounts with a connected store. Eligibility may start the following month and can vary by account. A standard manual order is another way to check a sample if the discount is unavailable.

Printful Review

Save the exact variant you plan to sell

In Design Maker, choose one T-shirt model, color and size. Save the template with your intended print placement. Include small lettering and a solid color from the real artwork so the sample can reveal legibility and color issues. Save the mockup for comparison.

Check checkout and delivery separately

In the dashboard, open New order and choose Test your design if your connected store shows that option. Confirm the product, delivery address, shipping quote and tax before submitting. Keep the product charge and shipping on separate lines. After delivery, photograph the print in daylight, check alignment, measure the garment and compare it with the published size guide. Record defects before washing, then check the result after one wash according to the garment instructions.

Use regular costs for the retail price

In a hypothetical order, a $28 sale minus $13 fulfillment, $5 shipping and $1.20 storefront and payment fees leaves $8.80 before returns, taxes and other costs. Use your regular quote for the same market and variant. The sample discount applies to a test purchase and should be excluded from your retail calculation.
