How to pre-sell your first course on Teachable

Build the sales page, set delivery expectations and check what students see immediately after checkout.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Make the timeline visible before payment
  3. Publish only what students should see
  4. Check the path with a test student
  5. What if the schedule slips?

Quick answer

A Teachable pre-sale collects payment before the full course is available. Explain what students can access immediately, when the rest unlocks and how they can reach you. Then walk through the checkout, thank-you page and welcome lesson as a test student.

Teachable Review

Make the timeline visible before payment

Plan a fictional three-module course. Give buyers a welcome lesson immediately, then list actual calendar dates for each module in the sales copy. Include the time zone if access is scheduled to the hour. Write down the price, billing frequency and refund terms before opening enrollment.

Publish only what students should see

Teachable’s Help Center article “Pre-sell a Course” describes keeping unfinished lectures in draft and publishing a welcome section. Put the schedule, contact channel and next step in that first lesson. Repeat the essentials on the thank-you page so students know how to get back to the school.

Check the path with a test student

Verify that the sales page clearly says pre-sale, checkout shows the expected amount and billing frequency, the thank-you page links to the school and draft lectures remain hidden in the student view. Use a preview or supported test for access checks. Confirm payment and receipt behavior separately with your payment provider’s approved test method.

What if the schedule slips?

Decide in advance when to notify buyers and how to apply your published refund policy. If your launch depends on reaching an enrollment minimum, explain the threshold and what happens if you miss it before taking payment.
