Teachable Starter vs Builder: how many course sales justify upgrading?

Compare standard monthly fees, platform fees and the Black Friday offer using a $79 course.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Compare the same billing period
  3. Try eight sales, then nine
  4. Check the Black Friday renewal before buying
  5. Check limits for the courses you intend to sell

Quick answer

At standard US monthly prices checked November 16, 2026, Builder becomes cheaper on the ninth $79 sale in one month if you use Teachable Payments and compare only subscription and platform fees. Your payment processing costs, refunds and plan needs can change the decision.

Teachable Review

Compare the same billing period

Starter is $39 per month plus a 7.5% platform transaction fee. Builder is $89 per month and charges a 0% platform transaction fee when you use Teachable Payments. Divide the $50 difference by 0.075 to get a break-even point of about $666.67 in eligible monthly sales. Use the actual amount charged after discounts in your own calculation.

Try eight sales, then nine

At eight $79 sales, Starter totals $86.40 and Builder totals $89. At nine sales, Starter is about $92.33 and Builder is $89. Those examples exclude processing fees, taxes, refunds and other charges. Use your last three months of sales to see whether you reach the threshold repeatedly.

Check the Black Friday renewal before buying

The November 16 US pricing page showed Builder at $44.50 per month for two months, then $89 per month. At the temporary price, the simplified break-even point is about $73.33 per month against the standard $39 Starter plan. Check eligibility, currency, payment availability and your actual renewal price at checkout. Recalculate at $89 before deciding to stay on Builder.

Check limits for the courses you intend to sell

The pricing table lists five products on Starter and ten on Builder. Count each product you need to list and preview one course as a student. If you need the higher product limit or another Builder feature, that may justify the extra fee before the sales threshold.
