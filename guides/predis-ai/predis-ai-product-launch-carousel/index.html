How to make a five-slide product launch carousel in Predis.ai

Turn one product brief into an Instagram carousel, then check facts, brand styling and generation credits.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Give each slide a job
  3. Check the slides, caption and link
  4. Measure the cost of an approved draft

Quick answer

Use Predis.ai when you have approved product facts and want an editable first draft. Its carousel maker accepts a brief or a product link and produces slides, caption copy and hashtags. Review everything before you publish or schedule a post.

Predis.ai Review

Give each slide a job

For a fictional travel mug, supply the confirmed capacity, approved photos and landing page. Ask for five slides covering the launch headline, use case, capacity, care instructions and a call to action. Provide care instructions yourself or leave that slide pending. Keep price, delivery promises and discounts out of the input until you have approved them.

Check the slides, caption and link

Apply your brand colors, then verify the product photo and factual claim on every slide. Preview the five slides in order on a phone. Keep headlines legible and make the last slide tell people where to go. Check the caption and hashtags for unsupported claims or irrelevant tags. Verify the destination separately, since a printed URL in the image cannot be tapped.

Measure the cost of an approved draft

Record credits before generation, after the first draft and after one revision. Divide the difference by the number of drafts you actually approve. Check the plan for scheduling and the credit cost for your format. Predis.ai describes editable slide downloads, so you can export and publish manually if your plan does not include automatic posting.
