Signaturely signing order: test two PDF signers and keep the audit record

Use a one-page approval sample to verify the signer order, field ownership and final archive.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Draw two blocks on a sample approval sheet
  3. Record what happens between the two signatures
  4. Save the evidence from the sender dashboard

Quick answer

If the client should sign before the internal approver, add both as recipients under Only Others and set a custom order. Signaturely’s documentation says the sender goes first in Me & Others. Confirm the two signature blocks and preserve the completed PDF with its Activity record.

Download the signing acceptance checklist

Signaturely Review

Draw two blocks on a sample approval sheet

Use a fictional sample named “Artwork approval — QA”. Add Client signature and date at the top, and Internal approval signature and date below. Assign each required field to its recipient. Use two mailbox addresses you control. Enter the client first and the internal approver second.

Record what happens between the two signatures

Before the client signs, verify that only the client has been prompted. After that signature, open the second inbox and verify that the internal approver can sign their assigned block. Compare the observed order with the CSV. Record the actual times in the same time zone if shown. After both sign, open the finished PDF and inspect both blocks. This is a proposed private rehearsal.

Save the evidence from the sender dashboard

As sender, open Documents → Options → Activity. Check that the document matches the approval sample and that the signature and completion entries appear. Save the signed PDF with the audit record. Signaturely documents a Business setting for a separate audit file on future requests. Otherwise check the merged download for the attached audit trail. No two-party request or audit export was run for this guide.
