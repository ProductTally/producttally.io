Signaturely signing order: send a PDF to two signers and save the audit trail

Set a signing order in Signaturely, assign the right PDF fields, and archive the completed file and its activity record.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Prepare a sample with two roles
  3. Verify the sequence from two inboxes
  4. Archive the activity record

Quick answer

In Signaturely, add each recipient to the request, set a custom signing order and assign fields individually. After completion, save the signed PDF and its audit record. Use an internal sample first so you can check delivery, field ownership and the final files before sending a client agreement.

Signaturely Review

Prepare a sample with two roles

Use a one-page fictional approval form with separate blocks for a client and an internal approver. If both roles are recipient accounts, choose Only Others and set the client first. Signaturely says the sender signs first in Me & Others, so confirm that choice before relying on a client-first workflow. Assign each signature and date field to its corresponding person.

Verify the sequence from two inboxes

Send the sample to two inboxes you control. Check whether the second recipient is prompted only after the first signs. Open each signing link separately and confirm it exposes the fields assigned to that recipient. After both complete, check the document status and keep the signed PDF.

Archive the activity record

Open the completed document’s Activity view. Verify that the recorded events match your test and save the audit record with the PDF. Depending on the selected setting, the record may be attached or downloadable separately. Signaturely documents Independent Audit Trail as a Business setting for future documents. Verify your plan and setting before promising a separate audit file.
