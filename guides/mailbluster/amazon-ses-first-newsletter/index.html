MailBluster with Amazon SES: a checklist for your first newsletter

Check sender identity, AWS Region and production access before testing your first MailBluster newsletter.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Match the sender and Region
  3. Confirm production access in the same Region
  4. Send a small internal test

Quick answer

If you send through your own Amazon SES account, verify your sender and confirm production access in the AWS Region connected to MailBluster. AWS allows limited direct SES sending in the sandbox. MailBluster says its SES integration cannot send test or bulk email while the account is in that mode.

MailBluster Review

Match the sender and Region

Write down the From address and the Region used by MailBluster. Check the email address or domain under SES identities in that Region. Verify the domain and inspect its DKIM status if you use domain authentication. Confirm that replies will reach an inbox someone monitors.

Confirm production access in the same Region

Request production access for your real newsletter use case and describe how subscribers grant permission and how you handle bounces and complaints. After approval, check the SES account status in the connected Region. If MailBluster still shows a sandbox message, refresh the connection status and compare Regions before changing any other settings.

Send a small internal test

Send to inboxes you control first. Verify sender name, reply handling, links, personalization fallback and unsubscribe on both desktop and mobile. Check the recorded delivery and suppression status before sending to your opted-in list. Keep a log of the identity, Region, template and test results.
