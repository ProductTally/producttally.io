pCloud Black Friday 2026: which Lifetime storage plan should you choose?

Compare 500 GB, 2 TB, 10 TB and the 3 TB bundle by your storage needs and checkout terms.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Estimate capacity from your files
  3. Compare the bundle with storage alone
  4. Check the checkout before committing

Quick answer

For pCloud Black Friday 2026, choose capacity from your current files plus expected growth. Consider the 3 TB bundle if you also want pCloud Pass and pCloud Encryption. pCloud’s November 10 announcement says the offer ends November 28, 2026. Check the price and included features for your region before paying.

pCloud Review

Estimate capacity from your files

Suppose you already have 620 GB and add 55 GB per month. In 12 months that is 1,280 GB before headroom. With a 25% buffer, plan for about 1,600 GB. A 500 GB plan is already too small and 2 TB fits this example. Use the total size of the material you intend to keep, including large exports and retained project versions.

Compare the bundle with storage alone

The announcement lists 500 GB, 2 TB and 10 TB Lifetime storage, plus a bundle with 3 TB, pCloud Pass and pCloud Encryption. Check whether you would actually use both extras before paying for the bundle. pCloud defines Lifetime as 99 years or the account holder’s lifetime, whichever is shorter.

Check the checkout before committing

Confirm capacity, currency, final total and included extras. If you are comparing with an annual plan, divide the one-time total by the annual price for comparable capacity to estimate the break-even period. Keep separate copies of irreplaceable files and practice restoring a sample before moving your whole archive.
