pCloud Black Friday 2026: which Lifetime storage plan should you choose?

Estimate whether 2 TB will cover a year, then compare pCloud’s storage plans and temporary 3 TB bundle.

Quick answer

Start with what you keep and how fast it grows. If you have 620 GB now, add 55 GB per month and want 25% headroom, 2 TB covers the next year. At 90 GB per month, the same archive needs 2,125 GB and exceeds 2 TB. pCloud announced a November 28, 2026 end date for its Black Friday offer.

pCloud Review

Make the 2 TB cutoff explicit

Size the archive with (current GB + 12 × monthly growth in GB) × 1.25. At 55 GB per month the result is 1,600 GB. At 90 GB per month it is 2,125 GB. With 620 GB already stored and 2 TB treated as 2,000 GB, the 25% buffer stops fitting above about 81.7 GB of monthly growth. These are illustrative calculations. Count exports, retained versions and originals you intend to keep.

Choose capacity before adding the extras

500 GB is already too small for either example. 2 TB works for the slower archive, while the faster one fits in 3 TB for the first year. pCloud’s November 10 announcement lists 500 GB, 2 TB and 10 TB standalone plans and a Black Friday bundle pairing 3 TB with pCloud Pass and pCloud Encryption. Compare the bundle and 10 TB on your longer-term storage estimate and the extras you will use. pCloud defines Lifetime as 99 years or the account holder’s lifetime, whichever is shorter.

Compare what you will actually pay

At checkout, confirm country, currency, capacity, included features and the final price. To compare one-time and annual billing, divide the one-time total by the annual total for equivalent capacity and add-ons. A hypothetical 300 total against 100 per year in the same currency is a three-year break-even before any changes to prices. Use the price you actually see. After November 28, check regular availability and pricing instead of relying on this promotion.

Try restoring the kind of file you care about

Upload a folder with a few files and a large export. Download it from another device, open the export and confirm the files you expected are present. If you intend to use Encryption, include a sample from the encrypted area in your check. Keep a separate copy of irreplaceable work before migrating the full archive.
