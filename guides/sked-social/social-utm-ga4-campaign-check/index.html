How to tag Sked Social links with UTMs and check campaign traffic in GA4

Use one naming convention, inspect the published link and check the GA4 traffic source.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Define one campaign name
  3. Inspect the published destination
  4. Check GA4 from the visitor’s side

Quick answer

Add your landing page to Sked Social’s URL field and choose a consistent UTM convention. Verify what visitors actually click, then check the session source and campaign in GA4. These campaign tags describe traffic to your site.

Sked Social Review

Define one campaign name

For a fictional launch, use utm_source=instagram, utm_medium=social and utm_campaign=november_launch. Use a different source for each platform and utm_content to distinguish variants. Keep spelling and case consistent.

Inspect the published destination

Sked says it applies default tags to links in its URL field and preserves existing tags. Inspect the live clickable placement, such as the bio link, and verify that any redirects preserve your values. If your network does not make caption URLs clickable, use a supported clickable placement.

Check GA4 from the visitor’s side

In a fresh session with consent and analytics enabled, open the link. Check Realtime for activity, then check Traffic acquisition after processing using Session source / medium and Session campaign. A click with no visible GA4 session can reflect consent, blocking, redirects or reporting delay. A UTM-tagged visit does not establish an affiliate sale.
