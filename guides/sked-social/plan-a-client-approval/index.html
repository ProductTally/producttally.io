How to plan a client approval workflow in Sked Social

Use a sample draft to plan internal review, client sign-off and scheduling, then compare the plan limits.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Separate the draft from its approval
  3. Check the review link and the plan

Quick answer

Sked Social documents an approvals board and client review links on Accelerate. We reached the 14-day trial dashboard on November 11, 2026. This guide follows the vendor workflow. Approvals and posting were outside the observed account setup.

Sked Social Review

Separate the draft from its approval

Prepare a sample post with a caption, an image and a proposed date. Define who checks the copy and who gives the final client sign-off. Sked’s approval board illustrates how a post moves through internal and external review.

Check the review link and the plan

Use a test reviewer when evaluating the client view and confirm what they can approve or return for edits. The official page lists three users and six profiles on Grow, and six users and ten profiles on Accelerate. A profile is a connected social account. Check the plan before connecting a client’s account.
