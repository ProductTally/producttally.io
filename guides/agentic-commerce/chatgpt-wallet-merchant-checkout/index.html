ChatGPT Wallet for merchants: a checkout readiness checklist

Walk a sample item from its product page through checkout, payment approval and order confirmation.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Product page and checkout
  3. Keep payment approval and the order separate
  4. Decide what counts as ready

Quick answer

Start with one item your store can fulfill. Check that its price and availability agree with checkout, that eligible customers can complete the normal order flow and that your store confirms the order. OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT Wallet in dots on November 13, 2026, for eligible US ChatGPT Pro users aged 18 or over with an eligible US-issued card and US billing address.

Product page and checkout

Use a fictional $29 desk planner. Note the selected variant, stock and price. In a test copy of the store, compare those details with the cart and the final total after shipping and tax. Verify what happens when the item goes out of stock or the total changes before checkout.

Keep payment approval and the order separate

OpenAI describes Wallet as using a substitute payment credential with the merchant’s existing checkout. A buyer’s approval authorizes a purchase attempt. Check your own order record for the outcome before preparing a shipment. If the attempt is declined or an order is missing, follow your processor’s normal support process.

Decide what counts as ready

In a sandbox or with a payment provider’s approved test flow, record the selected variant, order ID, payment status and confirmation message. Test an accepted order and a declined attempt. The accepted order should trigger one confirmation. The declined attempt should leave nothing to fulfill. This checklist can validate your store’s own checkout flow. Live Wallet compatibility requires an eligible account and a separate verification.
