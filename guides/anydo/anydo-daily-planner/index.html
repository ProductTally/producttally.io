What is Any.do for? Personal planning and light teamwork

See how Any.do connects a backlog, a daily plan and your calendar, and when a shared board is worth adding.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Give the backlog a place and the day a limit
  3. A shared board helps when work changes hands
  4. Decide based on how you plan

Quick answer

Any.do is a daily planner for people who need to turn scattered tasks into a manageable day. It fits independent creators and small teams with simple handoffs.

Any.do Review

Give the backlog a place and the day a limit

A creator might keep an episode, a newsletter and admin work in separate lists, then use My Day to choose what belongs in today’s plan. Checking the calendar before taking on another task makes the workload visible.

A shared board helps when work changes hands

If someone regularly takes over drafts or approvals, compare the team plan and its shared workspace. If the team needs formal approvals or a detailed project roadmap, evaluate those requirements separately. One G2 reviewer used separate calendar events to control how much time a task occupied.

Decide based on how you plan

Choose Any.do if the main question is what to do today. Confirm quick date entry in the language you actually use. The related publishing-week guide shows how our trial handled date entry and reminders.
