What is Submagic for? Choose between captions, finishing and clip selection

Find out when Submagic can handle a weekly series and when to keep your existing editor.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

Quick answer

Submagic is a browser editor for short spoken videos. It is a good fit when you already have clips and want a consistent caption style or faster finishing. For a long podcast, evaluate Magic Clips as a separate step. If you need precise audio or cuts, finish in your usual editor and test an SRT handoff.

Submagic Review

For a weekly talking-head series, try the captions workflow first

Say you publish three short tips a week. Bring in one approved cut, correct its brand names and numbers, and make a caption style you could reuse. If the timing and readability hold up on a phone, that is the part of the workflow worth moving. The official demo shows editing options. Our trial verified a corrected SRT on one English clip.

For a podcast, judge the selection before polishing

Give Magic Clips one interview segment and review the first three suggestions. Keep a candidate only if it starts with enough context, completes a thought and leaves the speaker’s point intact. If your editor already picks the excerpt, use captions and finishing on that selected clip. We did not measure the quality of suggested highlights.

Keep the usual editor when the cut or sound keeps changing

For a client video with a separate mix or several revision rounds, lock the cut before handing off the subtitle file. If a new edit moves a cut, recheck captions from that point onward. One Reddit creator reported playback or sync trouble after export; use that as a prompt to watch one actual file from the start, middle and end.

Estimate the work for a four-week series

At three uploads per week, 12 captions-only uploads cost 36 credits at the published rate. Doing silence removal and audio cleanup on all 12 gives an illustrative 84 credits. Magic Clips is listed separately at 8 credits per generated clip. Confirm features, export limits and credits in your plan.
