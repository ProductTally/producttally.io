How to sell a one-time digital product bundle on Payhip

Group three digital products, set one price and verify what a buyer receives.

By Evan · Updated Nov 13, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Define the outcome and price
  3. Set up one payment
  4. Verify every item

Quick answer

Use a Payhip Bundle with a one-time price when several existing digital products solve one job together. Test access to every included item from the buyer’s side before sending traffic to the bundle.

Payhip Review

Define the outcome and price

Imagine a launch kit with a planning template for $12, a checklist PDF for $8, and a graphics pack for $10. Bought separately they total $30. A hypothetical $24 bundle saves $6 compared with those three prices. State the price basis and list the exact deliverables, formats and compatibility.

Set up one payment

Under Products, choose Add new product, then Bundle. Name it and add the three existing products. In Pricing, choose a one-time payment, then edit the sales page to explain what each item does. Check that the checkout shows the intended amount and billing frequency before sending the link to buyers.

Verify every item

Use a separate buyer session and a controlled checkout test. Open the thank-you page and any receipt link, then open each of the three items with the buyer account. A no-charge test can check access; verify payment processing separately if you need that assurance.
