How to sell a Payhip bundle with one payment and check all three downloads

Price a $24 launch kit and use the receipt to verify that every promised file is accessible.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Show buyers precisely what the $24 includes
  3. Make the one-time option clear at checkout
  4. Open the spreadsheet, the PDF and the ZIP separately

Quick answer

Use a one-time Payhip Bundle when your existing products solve one job together. For a launch kit with a spreadsheet, PDF and ZIP, list exactly what buyers get. In a zero-charge rehearsal, verify all three items through the receipt. Payment approval needs a separate test.

Download the bundle delivery checklist

Payhip Review

Show buyers precisely what the $24 includes

Use an example launch kit: Launch Planner ($12, launch-planner.xlsx), Launch Checklist ($8, launch-checklist.pdf), and Launch Graphics ($10, launch-graphics.zip with three PNGs). At the example $24 bundle price, the discount is $6 on the three standalone prices. State the software and formats buyers need and which files are included.

Make the one-time option clear at checkout

Create a Bundle in Payhip, attach the three existing products and add a one-time price under Pricing. Open the sales page in a clean browser and confirm it lists all three products and advertises a single $24 payment. Apply your limited test coupon only after checking the price. The rehearsal should show $0 and should use a buyer email you control.

Open the spreadsheet, the PDF and the ZIP separately

Start from the test receipt using the buyer session. Open the spreadsheet and find the launch plan tab, open the PDF and find the prelaunch checks, then extract the graphics ZIP and count three PNGs. Mark each item in the downloaded checklist. A working receipt does not establish that every file is accessible. This is a proposed acceptance test based on Payhip’s documentation; no paid checkout was run.
