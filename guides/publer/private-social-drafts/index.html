Publer: How to save and reuse multilingual social drafts in Publer

Keep multilingual captions and campaign links in one private idea, then reuse the copy when it is time to prepare a social post.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Write the variants together
  3. Prepare the post in the composer

Quick answer

In a free-account check on November 11, 2026, English, Chinese and Japanese copy and a URL survived a reload in a private idea. The same idea opened in the composer.

Publer Review

Write the variants together

Add a campaign name, a separate paragraph per language and the full destination URL. Choose Private and save. Reload and compare the text and URL to your original.

Prepare the post in the composer

Open the saved idea in the composer and select the language variant for that audience. Check the URL and network formatting before scheduling. This workflow stopped before connecting accounts or posting.
