Publer for small teams: turn social post ideas into a publishing calendar

Keep language variants together in Ideas, then prepare each post for its own network and audience.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Write the variants together
  3. Prepare the post in the composer
  4. Run one campaign across two channels

Quick answer

Publer fits creators and small teams managing several social accounts. Use Ideas for drafts, the composer for channel-specific edits, and the calendar for scheduling. In a free-account check on November 11, 2026, English, Chinese and Japanese copy and a URL survived a reload in a private idea. The same idea opened in the composer.

Publer Review

Write the variants together

Add a campaign name, a separate paragraph per language and the full destination URL. Choose Private and save. Reload and compare the text and URL to your original.

Prepare the post in the composer

Open the saved idea in the composer and select the language variant for that audience. Check the URL and network formatting before scheduling. This workflow stopped before connecting accounts or posting.

Run one campaign across two channels

For a new guide, start with one private idea containing the destination URL and a draft for each audience. In the composer, prepare separate posts for two channels. Check the media crop, caption and link placement for each, then review their scheduled times together in the calendar. The official plan page lists supported networks and post types. Check your exact combination before connecting accounts.

An incentivized TrustRadius review from a small team liked scheduling across several Instagram and X accounts and found it less convenient to repeat evergreen posts in bulk. If repetition is central to your work, try one complete cycle with a low-risk post. For a single account with occasional posts, a native scheduler may be enough.
