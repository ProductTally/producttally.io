What is Publer for? Social scheduling for small teams

Decide when one calendar for several accounts and channel-specific drafts is worth adopting.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Centralize the calendar while keeping posts channel-specific
  3. Check formats and repeat scheduling
  4. Start with the scheduling problem you already have

Quick answer

Publer helps creators and small teams organize ideas and schedule posts across social accounts. It fits teams that repeat the same campaign across channels, languages or audiences.

Publer Review

Centralize the calendar while keeping posts channel-specific

Keep an announcement and its variants in Ideas, prepare the posts in the composer and use the calendar to spot gaps or clashes. This is useful when the same campaign needs different copy and media on different networks.

Check formats and repeat scheduling

The official plan page lists supported networks and post types. An incentivized TrustRadius review valued multi-account scheduling and found bulk recurrence less convenient for evergreen posts. Check recurring formats against the actual platforms you use.

Start with the scheduling problem you already have

A single account that posts occasionally may do well with its native scheduler. If you coordinate several accounts, the shared calendar is a stronger reason to consider Publer. The related draft guide covers saving language variants and opening them in the composer.
