How to check a citation year and export a correction with AnswerThis

Use the DOI to identify the paper, compare the year with the publisher and export the corrected reference.

By Evan · Updated Nov 11, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Check the DOI and the formatted citation
  3. Confirm the year at the publisher, then export

Quick answer

On November 11, 2026, AnswerThis matched the DOI 10.1109/CVPR.2016.90 and flagged a deliberately incorrect citation year of 2025. The corrected text export retained the DOI and changed the year to 2016.

AnswerThis Review

Check the DOI and the formatted citation

Open the public Reference Checker. Paste the DOI on one line and the formatted citation on another. In our example the DOI matched, and the second row flagged 2025 → 2016. Crossref and AnswerThis returned the match. PubMed did not confirm both entries.

Confirm the year at the publisher, then export

Open the DOI’s publisher record and compare the year, title and venue before accepting the suggestion. We selected Use year: 2016 and downloaded the corrected text. The test covered this reference pair. It did not assess research synthesis, retraction coverage or whether the paper supports a particular claim.
