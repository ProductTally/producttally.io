How to verify a citation year with AnswerThis and the publication record

Follow the same DOI from a deliberately incorrect year to a corrected text export.

By Evan · Updated Nov 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Quick answer
  2. Enter one identifier and one reference to expose the mismatch
  3. Confirm that the correction belongs to the same paper
  4. Export the correction, then inspect the actual text

Quick answer

AnswerThis flagged a deliberately wrong year of 2025 for DOI 10.1109/CVPR.2016.90 during our November 11 test. Crossref and AnswerThis matched the record to 2016. The exported correction retained the DOI. Check the original record before applying any change.

AnswerThis Review

Enter one identifier and one reference to expose the mismatch

In Reference Checker, put the DOI on one line and the citation with the deliberately wrong year on another. The test paper was Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition. The DOI-only result found the record; the citation result flagged 2025 against 2016. PubMed was unavailable on the first line and did not find the second. Those provider states do not override the matching records.

Confirm that the correction belongs to the same paper

Open the DOI or the official CVPR proceedings. Match the title, authors and venue before changing the year. If more than one work matches, keep the original line and resolve the identity first. A metadata match tells you about the publication record. Read the paper separately to check whether it supports the sentence in your draft.

Export the correction, then inspect the actual text

Select Use year: 2016 and export the corrected text. In our sample the year changed and the DOI stayed the same. Compare the export with the original for changes you did not select. Keep the original reference and a source note until your document has been checked. We tested this pair of inputs, without checking retraction coverage or bulk extraction.
