How to get started

  1. Choose a test page and save a distinct SEO title and description. Confirm both in the page HTML.
  2. On a page you intend to exclude, change the noindex setting and check the rendered robots directive and sitemap listing afterward.

Confirm the published robots directive and sitemap entry after the change. Use a staging page or a URL you intend to remove from indexing.
