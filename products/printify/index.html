Why choose it

  • Compare print providers before choosing who will make and ship your product.
  • Start with a small collection and add designs without buying inventory in advance.

How to get started

  1. Choose a product and print provider for your main delivery region, then upload one design.
  2. Compare product and shipping costs with your selling price, allowing for applicable taxes and storefront fees.
  3. Order a sample to check the print, sizing, and delivery experience before publishing to your connected store.
