Who it is for

Gemini Notebook fits research based on a set of sources you already have. Use it to compare a few documents, then open the cited passage to verify dates, numbers and context before you reuse them.

Google Help · Add sources

Product highlights

  • Select which sources to query and open a citation to inspect its supporting text.
  • A citation can point to a whole short source. Open the original before quoting a figure or a claim.
Google’s Gemini Notebook announcement Google Help · Add sources Google Help · Citation limitations Google’s public I/O 2026 notebook Google AI plans · United States

Why choose it

  • For creators and small teams turning a set of source documents into a research brief.

How to get started

  1. Create a notebook and add two or three PDFs you have permission to use. Keep the title and date visible so you can tell versions apart. Select the documents you want the chat to use.
  2. Ask for a table with one row per source and columns for publication date, the figure or policy, and its citation. Ask the notebook to identify disagreements and report missing information.
  3. Read the cited passage and check the unit, date and scope against the original document. If a citation opens a whole document, locate the passage yourself. Keep unresolved discrepancies in the brief.

Confirm that each figure has a source and that the brief preserves differences in date, unit and scope.
