Task & schedule management

Any.do

Turn a busy day into a clear plan. Any.do brings task lists, reminders and calendar events together, with My Day for choosing what to focus on today.

By Evan · Checked Nov 10, 2026

Any.do · Visit official website Save product Any.do Explore guides for this product

Product highlights

  • My Day gives you a private daily plan and is available on all plans.
  • Bring Google, Outlook or iCloud calendar events alongside your tasks.
  • Add tasks and organize your to-dos; advanced options depend on your plan.
Any.do · Product highlights Official guide

Why choose it

  • Keep today’s priorities in view instead of working through an undifferentiated backlog.
  • Plan tasks around your meetings with a shared view of events and to-dos.

How to get started

  1. Create a list and add the tasks you need to finish.
  2. Open My Day and choose the tasks to focus on today.
  3. Optionally connect a calendar and arrange tasks around your events.
