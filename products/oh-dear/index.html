Product highlights

  • Monitor authorized websites for availability, certificate issues, DNS changes and broken links. Results follow each check’s schedule.

Why choose it

  • Bring availability and site-integrity signals together, then use the full URL and HTTP response to diagnose the affected page.

How to get started

  1. Add a website you own or have permission to monitor.
  2. Choose checks and notification routes, send a test notification and review the latest results.
