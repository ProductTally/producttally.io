Plagiarism checker

Quetext

Quetext is an online plagiarism checker that highlights passages with possible matches and links to their sources. Writers and editors can use the report to review quotations and citations before publication.

Who it is for

For writers and small editorial teams reviewing drafts before publication. Start with an excerpt you are authorized to submit and compare flagged text against the original sources.

What to consider

The free plan covers up to 1,000 words in the plagiarism checker. The match score is a prompt for editorial review, not a verdict of plagiarism.
