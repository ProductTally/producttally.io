Who it is for

Coupler.io fits recurring reports whose formulas should stay separate from imported data. Send a sample into a dedicated Google Sheets tab and check whether the next run should replace rows or append them.

Product highlights

  • Coupler.io connects GA4 to Google Sheets and supports replacing or appending rows. The Free plan offers manual refresh and up to 100 source rows per run.

Why choose it

  • A dedicated data tab lets a small team refresh the same report without rebuilding its formulas.

How to get started

  1. Select one GA4 property and the last seven complete days. Choose only the fields the report needs.
  2. Send one run to a dedicated raw-data tab, and keep formulas on a separate tab. Pick replace or append according to how the report should refresh.

Refresh once and compare the dates, row count and one GA4 metric before allowing the spreadsheet formulas to depend on the import.
