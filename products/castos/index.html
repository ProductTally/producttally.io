Who it is for

Castos fits podcasts that want WordPress to remain the publishing hub. Start with one episode and verify playback through its RSS feed. Decide who should have access before distributing a private feed.

Castos · Official guide

Product highlights

  • Host media and publish a podcast RSS feed. Choose public, private or hybrid visibility; individual subscriber feeds allow access to be revoked.
Castos · Product highlights View official plans and pricing Official guide Product image source

Why choose it

  • Manage a WordPress-led podcast through Seriously Simple Podcasting, keeping publishing and your website connected.

How to get started

  1. Prepare the show title, description, square artwork and an MP3.
  2. Create the show, upload an episode and publish or schedule it; submit its RSS to listening directories.

Open the feed in a compatible reader and play the episode. If the show is private, check access with the intended subscriber feed.
