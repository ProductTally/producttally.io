Who it is for

Video creators and localization teams producing short multilingual voiceovers.

Commercial use requires audio generated under an eligible paid plan. Listen to each language before publishing.

Product highlights

  • The web Text to Speech editor detects the language from your text. The voice you choose affects the accent.
  • The free plan is for noncommercial use with attribution. Commercial use requires generating the audio under an eligible paid plan.

Why choose it

  • For creators and small teams making short voiceovers in several languages.

How to get started

  1. Write one short script in three languages. Keep the same names and numbers so you have comparable checks.
  2. Paste one version into Text to Speech, choose a voice suited to the target audience and generate a sample. Repeat for the other scripts before editing the video.

Listen without music first. Mark pronunciation, omissions and pauses against the script for each language.
