Why choose it

  • Draft the visual and caption together so you can review the whole post before approving it.
  • Use the brand kit to give future drafts a consistent starting point for colors and typography.

How to get started

  1. Write a brief with the product, audience, and one action you want readers to take.
  2. Generate one post. Check the copy against your product facts, then adjust the design in the editor.
  3. Check the credits used after any regeneration, then download the approved post or schedule it on a supported channel.
