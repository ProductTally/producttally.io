Why choose it

  • Preview titles and transitions on your footage before adding them to the timeline.
  • Start with a blank timeline for precise cuts, or use a template with placeholders for clips and titles.

How to get started

  1. Import a few clips and arrange them on the timeline.
  2. Split or trim unwanted footage, then add a title and adjust the music level.
  3. Export a short MP4 and play it back to check the cut, title readability, and audio balance.
