Todoist

Turn loose intentions into tasks you can actually act on.

By Evan · Checked Nov 6, 2026

Who it may suit

Individuals who want quick task capture and are willing to check the parsed date before saving.

What we checked

In the Chinese interface, a Chinese phrase for tomorrow at 10:00 and P2 was parsed correctly. We saved, completed, and reloaded the task; the inbox remained empty.

What still needs a closer look

An English date phrase was not recognized in the Chinese interface. Recurring tasks, collaboration, cross-device sync, and paid reminders have not been tested.
