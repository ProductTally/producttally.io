Frase

Turn a content question into a research brief and an editable first draft. Frase connects keyword exploration, content planning and Markdown export for small teams with an editor checking the facts.

By Evan · Checked Nov 11, 2026

Product highlights

  • Select a research language and market, build an editable brief, import an article URL or generate a new draft.
  • Export research, briefs and article drafts as Markdown.
Frase · Product highlights View official plans and pricing

Why choose it

  • Keep the research context and outline close to the draft, then review the exported text in your own editorial process.

Before you choose

Search-volume data can be missing, source labels need checking, and an audit can cover only a sample of discovered pages. Existing-article optimization encountered repeated version conflicts in the recorded trial; new-draft success does not establish reliable automatic rewriting. Planning ratios need editorial validation.

How to get started

  1. Select a research topic, language and market.
  2. Review keyword gaps and source links before creating the brief.
  3. Generate a draft, verify claims and attribution, then export for editorial approval.
