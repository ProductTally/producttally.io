Why choose it

  • A one-source preview gives a small team a concrete report to compare with the original numbers.

How to get started

  1. Connect one marketing account and select a short period already visible in the source.
  2. Choose a small set of fields and inspect the preview before sending anything to Sheets. Keep the source account open for the comparison.

Compare one metric over the same date range and aggregation in both the source account and the preview.
