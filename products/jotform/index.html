Jotform

Good questions. Useful answers.

Build forms to turn feedback into something you can work with.

By Evan · Checked 2026-11-06
J

What we checked

We built a bilingual feedback form, submitted Chinese, English, and Spanish sample values, and verified the backend record and downloaded CSV. A 390px layout kept fields and submit button visible.

What still needs a closer look

Conditional logic, spam protection, notification delivery, integrations, and payment forms have not been tested.

Who it may suit

Creators collecting simple multilingual feedback who need to keep a portable copy of the responses.
