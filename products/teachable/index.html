Who it is for

Teachable suits independent educators and small teams turning a workshop, tutorial series, or professional skill into a paid course. Compare the subscription and transaction fees at your expected sales volume before choosing a plan.

Teachable online course features

Product highlights

  • Organize a course into sections and lessons, and add video, text, and downloadable materials.
  • Set a course price as a one-time purchase, payment plan, or subscription, or give learners free access.
  • Starter adds a 7.5% platform transaction fee. Builder and Growth list 0% when using Teachable Payments. Payment processing fees may still apply.

Why choose it

  • Give students one place to enroll, pay, and return to their lessons.
  • Offer installments for a higher-priced course or use a free course to introduce your teaching.

How to get started

  1. Create a course and outline what students should achieve in each section.
  2. Upload a sample lesson and preview it as a student to check the video and downloads.
  3. Set the price, write the sales page, and review checkout before sharing the course.
