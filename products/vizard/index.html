Why choose it

  • Review several candidate cuts from the same interview before choosing which one to develop.

How to get started

  1. Upload an interview, podcast, or webinar recording you have permission to reuse.
  2. Generate clip suggestions, or select the quote you want in the transcript. Include enough of the speaker’s setup for the clip to stand on its own.
  3. Set the output to 9:16 for Shorts or Reels. Check the crop and correct names in the captions before exporting.
