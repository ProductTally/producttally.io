Why choose it

  • Reuse the same reference when comparing two ways to frame or move through a scene.
  • Choose the look of a draft shot before generating the longer sequence.

How to get started

  1. Open Cinema Studio 4.0 and describe one shot. Add a product photo or a location reference you have permission to use.
  2. Select a lens, lighting setup, and camera move. Review the credit cost before generating.
  3. Compare the draft with your reference for product details and framing. Change one camera setting before the next take.
