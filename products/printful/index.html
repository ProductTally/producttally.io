How Printful fulfillment works

Product highlights

  • Printful produces and ships items as orders come in. Orders from a connected store can be sent to Printful for fulfillment.
  • Use Design Maker to place artwork on a product, preview a mockup, and save a template for your store.
  • The base product price covers the item, one design placement, and fulfillment. Shipping, taxes, and extras such as additional print areas can add to the total.

Why choose it

  • Prepare a small collection without buying a batch of stock upfront.
  • Preview designs in mockups and order a sample to inspect the actual print and fit.

How to get started

  1. Choose one product and upload your artwork to Design Maker. Check its size and placement, then save the mockup.
  2. Connect a supported store and add the product. Compare your selling price with the product cost, shipping, taxes, and any store fees.
  3. Order a sample to your main market before launching. Check the print, fit, packaging, and delivery time.
