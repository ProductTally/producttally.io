Product highlights

  • Restream can send a single live feed to several connected channels.
  • In Studio you can invite a guest by link and add them to the show from backstage.
  • Free supports two simultaneous channels with branding. Facebook Pages and custom RTMP destinations require a paid plan.
Restream live streaming and Studio Restream plans and channel limits

Why choose it

  • Reuse a single production for multiple destinations.
  • Start in the browser or use your existing streaming software.

How to get started

  1. Connect the channels you want to reach and check eligibility on each platform.
  2. Open Studio or configure your streaming software. Verify sound and video during a rehearsal.
  3. Enable only the destinations for this event. Check the viewer feed on each one.
