Why choose it

  • A fit for small SaaS teams that can configure a sending domain and connect signup or subscription events.
  • Use simulation to review the path a sample contact would follow before activating a sequence.

How to get started

  1. Draft a welcome email and one follow-up for a single signup event.
  2. Set a goal such as subscription activation and simulate the sequence with a sample contact.
  3. Verify the sending domain and approval status before activating the sequence for opted-in contacts.

Run two sample contacts through the sequence. One activates before the follow-up, while the other stays eligible for it. Confirm both paths before enabling sends.
