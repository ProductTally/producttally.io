Who it is for

Choose Udemy when you want a self-paced course for a specific creator skill. Buy one course for a focused learning goal; compare Personal Plan only after checking whether the exact courses you want are included. Small teams can evaluate Business Team against their seat count and annual budget.

Product highlights

  • An individual course purchase applies to that course. Udemy’s plan comparison describes lifetime access to individually purchased courses.
  • Personal Plan provides access to a curated course collection during the subscription; it does not include every marketplace course.
  • The Udemy Business Team page lists 2–50 people. Its US page showed $360 per user per year, before applicable taxes, when checked November 12, 2026.

Why choose it

  • Choose a single course when you have one specific skill to learn and want access to that course after the purchase.
  • Compare a subscription or Team plan when several included courses or shared team learning would make better use of the plan.

How to get started

  1. Write down one outcome you want to produce, such as a publishable video, a landing-page draft, or a repeatable marketing workflow.
  2. Compare the course preview, curriculum, instructor, last-updated date, language, and stated prerequisites.
  3. Check whether the exact course is included in Personal Plan in your region, then compare the current local checkout price and access terms.
