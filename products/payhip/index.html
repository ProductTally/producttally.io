Why choose it

  • Share a product or checkout link from a newsletter, social profile, or existing site.
  • Use the same storefront features on every plan and compare plans by monthly cost and platform fee.

How to get started

  1. Add a Digital Product and upload the files buyers should receive.
  2. Set the title, price, description, and cover. Choose Unlisted while preparing the launch.
  3. Set up an eligible payment provider and use a 100% coupon to check the buyer receipt and download link.
