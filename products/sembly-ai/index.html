Product highlights

  • Sembly can record supported Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex meetings and generate a searchable transcript and notes.
  • Meeting notes include a summary and next steps. You can also upload an existing recording.

Why choose it

  • Return to the transcript to check a disputed decision.
  • Review suggested owners and deadlines before updating a project board.

How to get started

  1. Use a short call that participants have agreed may be recorded, or upload an authorized recording.
  2. Compare the draft transcript and notes with two decisions you remember.
  3. Confirm names, owners, and due dates against the transcript before sharing the recap.
