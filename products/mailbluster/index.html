Why choose it

  • Build email layouts in the drag-and-drop editor and preview them before sending.
  • Choose managed delivery or use an existing supported provider such as Amazon SES or Postmark.

How to get started

  1. Choose a sending option and set up your sender details.
  2. Create a campaign, select your audience, and add a subject and content.
  3. Send a test to your own inbox and check the layout and links before scheduling the campaign.

Use Send test email to check the message in an inbox before sending it to the selected audience.
