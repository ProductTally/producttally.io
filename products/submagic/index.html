Who it is for

Video creators and small teams that regularly turn interviews or talking-head recordings into shorts.

Check the plan’s clip-length and export allowances against your typical footage.

Product highlights

  • Generate captions from your video and correct the wording in the editor.
  • Choose caption styles and adjust the presentation to suit your video.
  • Upload, edit and export from a browser; video limits and export options depend on your plan.
Submagic · Product highlights Official guide Product image source

Why choose it

  • Keep caption text and styling together while preparing a short video.
  • Make key phrases easier to notice with a consistent caption style.

How to get started

  1. Create a caption project and upload your video within your plan’s limits.
  2. Select the spoken language, generate captions and review names and wording.
  3. Choose a style, preview the result and check your plan’s export options.

Read names and numbers in the preview, then open a short export on the device where you will publish.
