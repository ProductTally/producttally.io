Submagic

A browser editor to evaluate for turning talking-head clips into captioned vertical videos.

By Evan · Checked Nov 10, 2026

Who it may suit

Creators and small video teams that want to test a repeatable short-video workflow before paying for a subscription.

What we checked

We uploaded the same 13.8-second vertical clip with synthetic English speech from two entry points. Both projects stayed at 98% for over five minutes and were editable on a later visit. Both rendered “Tally” as “L”. We corrected one, switched it to the Nora template and verified the correction in the downloaded SRT.

What still needs a closer look

We did not measure completion time or general transcription accuracy. The video export prompt produced no rendered file. An SRT contains caption text and timing, so it does not verify styling in a rendered video.
