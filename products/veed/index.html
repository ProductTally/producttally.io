VEED

Browser-based video editing, from the timeline to an actual file.

By Evan · Checked Nov 6, 2026

Who it may suit

Creators trying short text-based video edits who can work within the free export limitations.

What we checked

We edited a five-second clip with English, Spanish, and Chinese text and inspected the downloaded H.264 MP4: 1280×720, 30fps, with a visible VEED watermark.

What still needs a closer look

This was a text-only clip. Subtitles, audio, transcription, long projects, collaboration, and paid quality levels have not been tested.
