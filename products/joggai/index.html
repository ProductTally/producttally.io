Why choose it

  • Start with your existing product page and keep the script editable.
  • Change the framing or narration to suit the channel you plan to use.

How to get started

  1. Paste a supported product URL and check the imported details and media.
  2. Choose a presenter and voice, then set the language, duration and aspect ratio.
  3. Review the script and preview. Check the price, claims, pronunciation and visual rights before publishing.

Compare the draft with the live product page. Correct prices and claims, and listen to names and measurements in the preview.
