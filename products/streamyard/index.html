Why choose it

  • Rehearse with a guest before opening the broadcast to viewers.
  • Bring a guest on screen and share a presentation from the same studio.

How to get started

  1. Create a broadcast and choose one supported destination.
  2. Copy the guest invitation link and check both microphones before going live.
  3. Run a short rehearsal. Check framing, audio and the stream at the destination.
