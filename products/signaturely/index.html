Why choose it

  • A fit for freelancers sending a few agreements a month and small teams standardizing repeat paperwork.
  • Keep the signed file and audit history together for client handoffs.

How to get started

  1. Start with a PDF or Word file and choose who needs to sign.
  2. Add the signers, assign fields and check the signing order before sending.
  3. After completion, save the signed file and its audit history with the client records.
