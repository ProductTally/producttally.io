Launch your first Spanish HelpCrunch article: translate the category before the page

Publish one end-to-end help path in Spanish, from the language picker and category to the article.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

HelpCrunch translations attach to pages in your knowledge base’s primary language. Publish the primary article first. Add Spanish in the translation-language settings, then translate its category and section before saving and publishing the article translation.

Choose an article customers can already find

Start with an article that answers a frequent question, such as changing a billing email. Make sure the current primary-language page already describes the exact setting and uses names the customer sees in the product. In Knowledge Base → Settings → Languages for translation, add Spanish and enter the localized knowledge base title.

  • Multilingual knowledge base

Translate the route readers take to the answer

Return to the primary article and switch the editor to Spanish. Translate the category and section as well as the article, so someone starting at the help center home can navigate to the instructions. HelpCrunch prompts for missing category or section translations before you save. Adapt screenshot captions and verify that links lead to content the customer can use in the new language.

  • Multilingual knowledge base

Verify the help center the way a visitor sees it

Open the published help center, switch language in the public picker and navigate from the category to the article. The Translation Report can help you find remaining gaps. Keep the primary page active while its translation should be visible: disabling the primary article also disables its translations.
