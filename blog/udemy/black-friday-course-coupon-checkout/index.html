Udemy Black Friday 2026: choose a course and check the coupon at checkout

Build a short list from Udemy’s Black Friday sale, match it to a project and verify the offer before you pay.

By Evan · Updated Nov 23, 2026

Start on Udemy’s official Black Friday and Cyber Monday page, choose a course you can use soon, and confirm the price and coupon at checkout. Udemy says platform promotions are applied automatically to eligible courses. An instructor code can have its own expiry and eligibility, so check the label and the final total.

Udemy · View product details

Buy for the task you will do next week

Suppose you send a client an Excel report every Friday. Before opening the sale page, write down the part that takes the most time. Maybe you already know how to filter a table, but matching exported order codes with a price list takes half an hour. That gives you a reason to look at lookup formulas and to skip lessons you already use.

Udemy’s official sale page groups courses into categories such as Python, Microsoft Excel and certification preparation. Open the public syllabus for a promising course and check whether it covers the exact operation and includes a practice file or exercise you can use. Preview a lesson if available and confirm the spoken language and captions separately.

  • Udemy Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale

Check where the discount came from

In its coupon FAQ, Udemy distinguishes a platform promotion from a code distributed by the instructor. If Udemy runs the promotion, the platform applies it to qualifying items when you proceed to checkout. If an instructor sends a code, follow its terms, then check what the cart says. A course displayed on a sale page may still need an eligibility check in your account.

If you want to buy only one course, read the item name before continuing. Look for the course you selected and its payable total. If you intentionally chose a subscription, look at the billing interval and renewal conditions separately. Make the purchase decision from the price, currency and terms displayed to you at the time.

  • Udemy coupon FAQ

Make the first lesson part of the purchase decision

Choose the one lesson you will open and schedule time for it. In the Excel example, your goal could be to reconcile five rows and identify the two SKU exceptions. If a bundle includes extra training, buy it when you can name what you need from the other courses too. The course you can apply this week deserves a place on the short list.
