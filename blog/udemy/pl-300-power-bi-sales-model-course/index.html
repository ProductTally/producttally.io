Choose a Udemy PL-300 course with a Power BI sales model

Use a three-row order table to check whether a PL-300 course teaches Power Query, relationships and DAX beyond chart formatting.

By Evan · Updated Nov 23, 2026

For a Udemy PL-300 course, look for a project that uses Power Query to prepare data, connects separate tables in the model and writes a DAX measure. Power BI charts become useful once you can explain why a filter changes a total. Microsoft’s PL-300 study guide also covers visualization, analysis, management and security. The exercise below targets the modeling work.

Udemy · View product details

Can the report still explain where $45 came from?

Imagine a shop sends you three order lines. The export gives SKU, quantity and the price charged per item. A separate Products sheet has one row per SKU with the category. The paid price belongs with the order line. If the catalog price changes tomorrow, the old order should still be worth the amount the customer paid.

Can the report still explain where $45 came from?
SKUQuantityUnit price paid
N012$15
M012$12
N011$15

Give N01 the category Notebooks and M01 the category Mugs in Products. Set the relevant numeric types in Power Query. Make a one-to-many relationship from Products to OrderLines using SKU. A category from Products should filter the appropriate order lines.

Give the chart a total you can check by hand

Use the measure Revenue = SUMX(OrderLines, OrderLines[Quantity] * OrderLines[UnitPricePaid]). Expected revenue in the toy data is $69. Filtering to Notebooks should show $45. If the report stays at $69, check that the visual uses Products[Category] and that the relationship connects the matching SKU fields in the model. That diagnosis is the skill the exercise is designed to teach.

The linked Udemy course lists Power Query and DAX in its public outline. Preview an available modeling or measures lesson before committing. Look for a point where the instructor builds a calculation, applies a filter and explains why the result changed. Use Microsoft’s current study guide for exam scope and the public course outline for what you will practice.
