When does CapCut’s 9:16 Auto Reframe need a manual edit?

Calculate what fits in a 9:16 crop and choose tracking, a two-person layout or a fixed crop.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Decide what the shot needs to preserve: one speaker, two people or a detail they are pointing at. CapCut Desktop documents options such as aspect ratio and tracking speed. Use Auto Reframe as a first pass, then watch the faces, gestures and captions in the vertical result.

Check a second before and after each speaker change and preview the final dimensions on a phone. Manually reposition or layer the shots where needed. This is a geometry example; check feature availability and the rendered result in your client.
