How to make the client sign first with Signaturely

Set a custom sequence for the client and your company’s countersigner before sending the document.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

Enable custom signing order in Signaturely, then put the client above your company’s countersigner. In an uploaded document you arrange the actual signers. When preparing a reusable template, arrange the roles and map them to the right people when you use it.

Decide who signs and who only needs a copy

A small agency sends a statement of work to a client’s project owner. The operations lead should countersign afterward, while the project manager only needs the completed file. Write down the two signer names and their roles before uploading. Keeping the project manager out of the signing sequence avoids assigning them a step they do not own.

Set up the order before placing fields

Open Sign for an individual file or Templates for a reusable document. After uploading the file and opening the document editor, add the signers or roles, enable custom signing and drag them into order. Before sending, inspect the preview with the roles in mind: client signature and date on the client line, company signature and date on the countersignature line. The order controls who goes first. Field assignment controls who fills each spot.

Make the next request easier

If this is your standard statement of work, use role names such as Client Signer and Company Countersigner in the template. Before the next send, check the email addresses and confirm the client is still first. Keep that short pre-send check even when both signature fields live on the same page.
