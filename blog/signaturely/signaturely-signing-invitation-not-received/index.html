The Signaturely invite never arrived. Who is due to sign?

Check the recipient, signing order and activity before sending a reminder or opening a new draft.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Suppose the client has signed but your internal approver says no email arrived. Open the file in My Documents. Look at each recipient and their status. If the document has a custom signing order, verify whose turn it is. Check the recipient address and find out whether they expected the first invitation or a later reminder.

Check recorded activity and link delivery

Open Activity on a completed document to check the audit trail and match the time, file and signer. If the correct recipient is still due to sign and reminders fail, use Generate Signing Link for that signer and send it through a channel you already know belongs to them. The original email stays linked to the document and still receives notifications. If the address is wrong, handle that first.
