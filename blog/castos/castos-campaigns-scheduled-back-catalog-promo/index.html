Add a dated event promo to old podcast episodes with Castos Campaigns

Give listeners of your back catalog a current invitation, with an end date and no re-editing of episode files.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

Use Castos Campaigns to put a recorded announcement before or after every audio episode of the selected show, including its older episodes. Castos plays it alongside the recording without changing the source file. Set an end date and use Growth or higher if you need to queue the campaign ahead of time.

The offer should make sense on an episode from last year

Imagine a design podcast running a January 20 workshop. Someone may hear the invite while listening to an interview from two years ago. Record one short clip that says what the workshop is, gives the full date and directs listeners to a short, stable URL. A pre-roll puts a deadline in front of a listener arriving at any episode. A post-roll fits a quieter reminder after the story ends.

Put the stop date next to the episode selection

Upload the clip, select the show and choose pre-roll, post-roll or both. Use the public registration deadline to set the end date. If tickets sell out before then, end the campaign early. Essentials includes one campaign at a time. Growth and higher adds advance scheduling and more campaigns. The feature covers every audio episode of the selected show, which matters if different seasons have different audiences.

Listen where a person actually hears the announcement

Review one older episode and a current episode on the listening surfaces you use. Check that the invitation names the workshop, is intelligible at the transition, and points to the right page. Treat distribution surfaces separately from the audio upload: the Castos Campaigns feature inserts your clip around the hosted episode, while the original recording stays available for future edits.
