Submagic: How do you keep bilingual captions from hiding a product demo?

Compare separate subtitle files and a stacked layout, then check the longest caption in the actual player.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Choose the delivery format first. Submagic lists a Mandarin and English setting for speech that switches languages in one recording. For viewers who need to select a language, test separate subtitle files. For viewers who need both languages on screen, preview both together. Check whether your editor can place two tracks on the same frame.

Submagic · View product details

Check the tallest caption against the demo

Try “Save it now” and “When the client opens the link, they will see the checklist” in both languages. In an illustrative 1080 × 1920 frame, interface content at y=1400–1680 and captions at y=1510–1730 overlap by 170 pixels. Shorten the caption, reposition the interface or move the captions.

  • Submagic supported caption languages

Test the player where people will watch

Submagic documents burned-in and SRT exports. Play the longest cue with the player controls visible. With two subtitle files, switch languages and confirm each track stays synchronized.
