Use Submagic Brand Kit to keep captions consistent across ten short videos

Make a reusable caption design and decide which adjustments belong to each video.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

Submagic Brand Kit can organize reusable templates, logos and colors for your videos. The current pricing page lists Brand Kit on Pro and Business. Build the look on one representative short, then check where the captions land on each cut.

Pick the first clip by what it needs to show

For a series of product demos, start with a short that contains both the presenter and a visible dashboard. Use that clip to decide the caption colors, the contrast against the background and where the logo fits. A plain studio close-up is easier to style, but it tells you little about the busy footage the template will be applied to.

Save the parts that should stay consistent

Open Brand Kit in the dashboard, create the reusable template and store the logo or colors you want to reuse. Name the template by series and format, for example “Product Demos 9:16.” Apply it to a second project whose subject sits on the other side of the frame. Keep recognizable type and colors, and review caption position in the new composition.

Check the exports for what the template cannot see

A saved design cannot tell whether a caption covers today's product price, an interface button or a speaker's face. Before exporting the batch, look at short moments with a dense sentence, a name and a visible on-screen action. Adjust the affected clip, keeping the common design in the Brand Kit. Check the pricing page for your plan's template allowance if you need distinct versions for more than one brand.
