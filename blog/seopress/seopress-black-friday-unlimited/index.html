SEOPress Black Friday 2026: is PRO Unlimited worth it for your WordPress sites?

Count the sites that need PRO, work out the cost per site, and check how SEOPress renews the Black Friday offer.

By Evan · Published Nov 26, 2026 · Updated Nov 26, 2026

SEOPress lists PRO Unlimited at US$99 per year for Black Friday 2026, with the discount applied at checkout through December 2 at 11:59 p.m. CET. If you support multiple WordPress sites, count the ones that actually need PRO before you choose a license.

Budget for the sites you will actually maintain

Suppose you run a company site and support two client sites. Write down what each needs, such as structured data or redirect management. If only one needs PRO, price the single-site option shown at checkout. If you cover all three with the US$99 offer, your internal allocation is US$33 per site per year. You still pay one annual Unlimited charge.

Put renewal ownership in the client handoff

Record who owns the license, who can access billing, and who takes over if you stop maintaining a client site. SEOPress says the purchase price carries forward while automatic renewal remains on. Save the checkout currency, applicable tax, renewal setting, and order confirmation together. That gives the next maintainer something concrete to check.
