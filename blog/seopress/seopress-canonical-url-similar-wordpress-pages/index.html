Three WordPress product pages look alike. Which URL should SEOPress declare canonical?

Choose the canonical for a regular product page, campaign copy and regional variant, then check the live HTML.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Send the campaign copy to the main URL as its canonical when the heading is the only change. A regional version with local currency, shipping and returns can keep its own canonical, with hreflang where applicable. Google uses canonical signals and can select another URL.

Check what visitors and Google receive

On the campaign page, open the SEOPress metabox and enter the main page's absolute URL in Advanced → Canonical URL. Save and clear relevant caches. Open the served source and confirm one valid rel="canonical" in the <head>, pointing to a working destination. Check the other two pages too, and make internal links and the XML sitemap consistent with the choice.

  • SEOPress canonical settings

When the regional pages are intended to appear separately, confirm that each version includes its own URL plus the other regional alternatives in its hreflang set. Use full URLs. If two same-language regional pages have substantially the same content, review Google’s guidance on selecting a preferred canonical before assuming both should stay independently indexed.

If the campaign URL should stay out of search, consider noindex on a crawlable page. Blocking it in robots.txt can prevent Google from seeing noindex or canonical. Use actual access controls for private content. In Search Console, compare the declared canonical and Google's selected URL.
