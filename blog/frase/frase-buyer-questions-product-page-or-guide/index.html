Frase question research: which buyer questions belong on a product page?

Turn a question list into a product FAQ and one genuinely useful supporting guide.

By Evan · Published Nov 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 27, 2026

Use Frase Question Research to collect how shoppers phrase a question. Then triage the results before writing. A question that changes a purchase decision belongs close to the relevant feature or price, while a multi-step task can have a guide of its own. Use the vendor's current sources for answers.

Sort questions around a purchase

For a video subscription, take three examples: “Which plan exports SRT?”, “How do I replace captions on an uploaded clip?” and “When does cancellation take effect?” Put the plan answer with the plan details. Give the export workflow a separate article if it needs steps and screenshots. Place the cancellation condition beside billing. For each answer, attach the most specific official page.

Keep the handoff small

Translate the intent, including the local wording for a plan or billing interval, rather than copying English query variants into every locale. Send the editor the three questions, locations and source links. Mark a question as open if the official documentation cannot confirm an answer.
