How to edit Japanese refund emails drafted with DeepL Translator

Check refund status, promised dates and the next action in three Japanese customer support examples.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Check what happened, when the customer will hear back and what they should do. DeepL documents plain and polite tones for supported translations into Japanese on DeepL Pro. Polite tone is a starting point. The billing record and team commitments determine the actual wording. DeepL’s help center says the plain and polite option is currently unavailable for Korean-to-Japanese translations. For that language pair, review the Japanese tone directly.

Refund: say which step is complete

返金手続きが完了いたしました。カードのご利用明細への反映時期は、カード会社によって異なります。

This says you completed the refund process. Posting to the card statement is still up to the issuer. Give a date only if the team can commit to it.

Delay: promise the next update you can deliver

確認に時間がかかり、申し訳ございません。調査結果にかかわらず、11月25日17時（日本時間）までに進捗をご連絡いたします。

This promises a progress update by November 25 at 17:00 Japan time. It leaves the repair date open.

Fix: name the exact action to retry

修正を反映いたしました。請求書の画面を再読み込みして、もう一度「PDFをダウンロード」をお試しください。解消しない場合は、このメールにご返信ください。

Use the Japanese label on the actual button. These fictional examples were edited by hand. Have someone familiar with the case verify the status, date, label and promise before sending. Handle account or billing data in the approved workflow.
