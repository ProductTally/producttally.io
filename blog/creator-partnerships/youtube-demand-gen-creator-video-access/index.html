How to give a brand access to your YouTube video for Demand Gen

Hand off the exact Short, agree on usage and share brand partner access in YouTube Studio.

By Evan · Updated Nov 23, 2026

Creators and brands use YouTube brand partner access to connect a specific video to the advertiser. It enables the brand to view relevant performance metrics and boost the video with the supported format. Agree on how the video may be used before connecting it. Google highlighted Creator partnerships boost again in its November 18 Demand Gen update.

A finished Short still needs a handoff

Suppose a creator posts a 30-second demo for a desk accessory. The brand wants to use the published Short in its holiday promotion for two weeks. Put the URL of that exact cut in the handoff, with the launch date, intended markets, length of use and the person who approves any copy changes. If there are two versions of the video, name the one the brand can use.

YouTube explains that brand partner access provides platform permissions and advises creators to discuss usage rights separately. Record who can view performance data and how long the campaign will run before sharing access.

  • YouTube Help — sharing brand partner access

Choose who initiates the request

In YouTube Studio, the creator can select an existing reported brand deal during upload or editing. Some other creator-initiated routes, including sharing a code, are for YouTube Partner Program creators. If the video is already published, the brand or its agency can initiate a request for that video. The creator then reviews and accepts it in the account that owns the upload.

Where several client channels or agency accounts are involved, ask the advertiser to identify the requesting account. Confirm the association against the original video URL so the media team is working with the same upload.

  • YouTube Help — sharing brand partner access

Hand the linked video to the campaign owner

In the campaign brief, record the video URL, who confirmed access, and the agreed publication and stop dates. Give the advertiser the territory list and a copy of the approved product claims. Google’s November announcement gives examples of holiday Demand Gen workflows with creator partnerships boost. The applicable options and outcomes depend on the campaign.
