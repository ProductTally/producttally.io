Someone replied. Will Pombo still send the next reminder?

Decide what to do with replies, hard bounces and unsubscribes, and verify who remains queued before resuming.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Pombo can stop follow-ups for a contact who reaches a configured event or segment goal. A regular inbox reply needs a check in your own setup. Use an approved account and a controlled address to see what remains queued before you resume.

Pombo · View product details

Separate a reply, a bounce and an opt-out

A trial user replies that the demo is booked. At that point, stop reminders about booking it. Pombo documents a sequence-wide pause and the option to stop sending after a configured goal. Its documentation leaves ordinary inbox replies unconfirmed as an exit trigger. If your workflow depends on that behavior, reply from a test inbox you control and see whether the next step stays queued.

Hard bounceCheck the address and confirm the contact is suppressed before considering any further send.

Pausing holds enrolled contacts at their current step. They resume when you unpause, so check who is still eligible first. A booking reply can end a particular reminder without changing the person's wider subscription preferences. Pombo documents how contact status controls suppression, including the different treatment of hard bounces and marketing opt-outs.

  • Pombo opt-outs and suppression

For a booked demo, Pombo’s documented stop options are a named booking event or entry into a segment that represents the booked state, with stopping enabled. Simulate the sequence to inspect enrollment and the proposed messages without sending. That simulation does not confirm how an ordinary email reply is handled in your setup.

Check the queue and sending prerequisites

Pombo lists account approval, permission to email the audience, a verified sending domain and any applicable weekly cap as sending gates. Test messages are subject to the gates too. Once your account is approved, use addresses you control to check what happens if someone replies while the next message is waiting. Review the queue again before resuming.
