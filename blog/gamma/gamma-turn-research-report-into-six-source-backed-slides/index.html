Your research report needs six Gamma slides. Which figures need a source on the slide?

Use the IEA's 2025 EV outlook to keep source links, sales denominators and forecast dates in a six-slide Gamma deck.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Keep enough context for each number to make sense on its own. “17 million” needs the year, geography and what was sold. If a slide cites the IEA's 2025 forecast, mark it as a forecast made at the time. Draft the evidence and claims before importing an outline or source material into Gamma.

Plan the six slides around your sources

  • Gamma import guide

With a finished PPTX, DOCX or PDF, Gamma 5 offers an Import route. To turn an outline into new cards, add the materials in the prompt and state which figures and citations must survive unchanged. Review the outline before you generate the deck.

The IEA's Global EV Outlook 2025 provides a historical example.

Plan the six slides around your sources
SlideClaim or questionEvidence to retain
1This covers sales in 2024 and the outlook published in 2025Report title, date and scope
2Global electric car sales exceeded 17 million in 2024Units, year and geography
3Electric cars made up more than 20% of new car sales globallySales denominator, separate from cars already on the road
4China sold more than 11 million electric cars in 2024China, units and year
5Which 2025 numbers were forecasts at publication?Publisher, date and assumptions
6Where can readers verify the figures?The summary and market trends chapter

For a regional chart, compare the same unit across markets. A unit count and a sales share don't belong on the same scale. Give source links meaningful labels.

  • IEA market trends

Check the exported presentation

After generating the presentation, check each figure against the source or a calculation you can show. Keep a short source on the slide and the full reference on the last slide. Open the actual PDF or PowerPoint export and check the links, dates and text for clipping. The figures here were checked against the report by hand. The six-slide layout is a proposed outline.
