Gamma presenter view: rehearse a three-card deck with speaker notes

Add private talking points to three cards and use Gamma’s timer to rehearse a client presentation.

By Evan · Published Nov 26, 2026 · Updated Nov 26, 2026

Gamma attaches speaker notes to cards and shows them to the presenter in Presenter View. Open Presenter View from the menu beside Present to see the current card and your notes together.

Official source

Gamma · View product details

Give each card one job

On a three-card client deck, state the client’s goal on the first card, describe the proposed deliverable on the second, and name the decision needed today on the third. Add reminders to the notes, such as pausing after the budget question, so the audience-facing card stays easy to read.

Share the right window

Keep your notes window private and pick the audience-facing window when screen sharing. Run the built-in timer during rehearsal and note which card takes too long. Gamma lets you double-click the notes in Presenter View to edit them, so you can shorten that card’s talking points while they are fresh.
