How do you keep a brand name recognizable in JoggAI translations?

Run a short two-language trial with one brand, version and price, then check audio, captions and regeneration.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Make a pronunciation guide and test the same 5–10 second script in English and Chinese. JoggAI lists languages and documents translation. Check which voices, edits and regeneration controls your account provides. The language list does not establish dictionary support.

JoggAI · View product details

Keep the spelling and the spoken cue separate

Use the fictional brand Navo, with the intended reading “NAH-voh”, version 3.2 and a price of US$19.90. Keep Navo in captions. If your editing mode accepts a spoken cue, try it without changing the displayed spelling. Check the decimal, currency and version on their own.

Repeat the check on a short export

Have a speaker of the target language listen to the first occurrence and the end of a sentence. Log the timestamp and any segment you regenerated. If the mode lacks a pronunciation control, try a shorter line or record the phrase separately. Check available voices and regeneration controls in the account.
