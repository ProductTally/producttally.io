Sembly AI: Sembly Studio: turn approved client meeting notes into a proposal

Give the proposal a clear scope and keep unresolved decisions visible before you share it.

By Evan · Published Nov 26, 2026 · Updated Nov 26, 2026

Sembly 3.0 keeps meeting records in Notes and creates proposals, reports, and decks in Studio. You can bring meeting content and other materials into a proposal. Check your workspace’s Studio access and generation credits first, especially on a legacy plan.

Give Studio notes that define the scope

Imagine a small retailer planning a launch page. The client approved one page and two mobile hero options, but nobody has agreed who supplies product photos. Put those decisions and approved brand material in the brief. Ask for objectives, deliverables, inputs the client must provide, and open items. Leave prices or dates open if nobody agreed them.

  • Official source

A proposal brief you can adapt

Draft a launch-page proposal from the approved meeting notes. Include one landing page and two mobile hero options. List what the client needs to supply and mark product photography as open. Leave price and dates blank unless the notes confirm them.

Review the four lines a client will act on

Check the number of deliverables, acceptance criteria, owners, and unresolved questions. If the draft adds photography or a firm delivery date, move it to the open items unless the client has approved it. Once the promises match the meeting record, share the proposal. For multiple client brands, verify the workspace’s branding and credit options.
