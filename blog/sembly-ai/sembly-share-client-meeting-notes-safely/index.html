Sembly AI: The recording kept going after the client left. What should you share in Sembly?

Check what a Sembly Guest Link exposes and prepare a separate recap for your client.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Send a separate, reviewed recap and keep the original meeting restricted to the team. A Sembly Guest Link can expose the recording, transcript and key items. A password restricts who can open the link. It does not remove the internal conversation from the recording. Copy the client-facing decisions into a separate document or email, using an export option available in your account if helpful.

What the client actually needs to confirm

  • Sembly Guest Link access
  • Sembly meeting notes and exports

Imagine a 30-minute handoff call. In the first 24 minutes, both sides agree on a November 27 first draft and the client promises brand assets. After the client leaves, the team discusses its minimum renewal price. A recap could say:

We'll share the first draft on November 27. Please send the vector logo and brand colors by November 24. Once we have them, we'll confirm the schedule. The next review covers the homepage. We'll share dates for the other pages once the homepage is approved.

Review the new document's comments, permissions and links as well as its text. A timestamp may still point back to the original meeting. Keep internal source timestamps in the team's own notes.

When you really do need to share the full recording

Agree on the scope before sharing. Sembly's help explains the password option and how edit access affects the setting that restricts copying the Guest Link. Open the proposed link from an independent guest session and check the recording, transcript and key items as that guest. If a full-meeting link was already sent by mistake, review its access settings, test the old URL from a guest session and check separately shared copies or attachments.

  • Sembly Guest Link access

Before sharing the recap, open it in a signed-out session or a controlled guest account. It should open on its own and contain only the customer-facing decisions. If you change guest-link access, try the original link again. Sembly’s documented setting covers who can copy the link. Check actual access before treating a previously shared link as closed.
