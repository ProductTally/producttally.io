ElevenLabs: How do you fit AI narration to a 60-second product demo?

Align script length and voice settings with the key actions in a one-minute product demo.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Give viewers enough time to see each key action, then write the narration for that shot. When they need to find Share, lose the extra sentence and let the pointer rest briefly on the control. If the narration still runs long, compare the actual time it takes after rewriting or changing speaking speed before extending the shot. ElevenLabs describes speed settings and model-specific pause controls.

Time a small section first

Start with the shot that has the most to explain. Shorten its narration to the essential action and review that segment in your chosen voice.
