One SaaS plan charges per seat, another by usage. How do you compare them?

Calculate monthly costs, annual commitment and the break-even point for the same five-person team.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Hold the team, needed features and monthly volume constant. Record the pricing unit, payment period and included usage together. Stripe documents quantity-based and usage-based models. The prices below are fictional and are not Stripe fees.

Estimate costs for the same team

  • Stripe quantity pricing
  • Stripe usage-based billing

Plan for five people, USD before tax800 uses/month1,600 uses/month
A: $12 per seat/month, $720 paid annually, no usage surcharge6060
B: $0.06 per use, monthly, no minimum, all five can use it4896
C: $45 per workspace/month for 1,000 uses, $0.05 per extra use4575

C is cheapest at 800. A is cheapest at 1,600 on a monthly equivalent but commits $720 upfront. A and C both cost $60 at 1,300 uses. Check whether unused seats can be refunded or changed at renewal. Find out whether attempts, successes or retries count.

Add contract terms before choosing

For a real quote, record the vendor URL, market, currency, date and purchase terms. Exclude plans that lack required features. Recompute storage or editor charges at your own volume, then see whether the contract and cash flow suit the team.
