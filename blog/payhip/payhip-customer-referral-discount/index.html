How to set up a customer referral discount in Payhip

Set up a customer referral on an eligible Payhip product and explain how the friend’s coupon and the referrer’s reward work.

By Evan · Published Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

In Payhip, go to Marketing → Referral → Add Referral, select an eligible product and set the percentage. A shopper who uses a referral link receives a single-use coupon. Once that shopper buys, Payhip emails a coupon to the person who referred them. Payhip documents referrals for digital and physical products. Its referral feature excludes tiered products such as courses and memberships.

Pick a product the original shopper has a reason to buy

Imagine you sell a USD 24 set of printable craft-market signs. Customers sometimes send the listing to other stallholders, so choose that one product and give referrals a 15% discount. In this example a friend pays USD 20.40 before tax, and the referrer earns a 15% coupon after a qualifying purchase. The reward is a discount coupon. Tell shoppers what it applies to and when it expires.

Use the percentage to decide whether a successful referral makes sense for the offer. At 15%, set aside USD 3.60 per friend checkout before fees and taxes. Also consider the coupon the referrer can redeem. Use Payhip’s documented example when explaining accumulation: at 25% per referral, one purchase earns its example referrer 25%, and a second purchase raises that to 50%. Keep that example distinct from your own percentage.

Put the two clocks in the instructions

Under the documented flow, the friend has 24 hours to redeem their one-use code. The referrer has one week to use their earned coupon, and another successful purchase refreshes that period. You can communicate both times in a message such as “Your friend uses their code within 24 hours. When they buy, we email you a reward you can use for one week.” Link to the item that actually participates.
