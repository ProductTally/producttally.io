What to give away when you sell templates on Payhip

Help readers finish one quote for free, then give them a paid pack to manage deposits, balances, and changes.

By Evan · Published Nov 17, 2026 · Updated Nov 17, 2026

On this page

  1. Check access to checkout before building the store
  2. Give away a quote and sell the tools for the job that follows
  3. Use two addresses and check both sets of files
  4. Use real orders to decide whether the bundle is worth keeping

A reader asks for your quote sheet. You can give them a file they can use today and put a bigger project pack up for sale. First, check that Payhip can connect to a payment processor you qualify for. Payhip requires that connection even for a free download. Once checkout works, the question is what the free sheet should finish and what extra work the paid pack should take off the buyer’s plate.

Payhip · View product details

Check access to checkout before building the store

Open Account → Settings → Payment Details and check the providers for your business location. Payhip allows personal PayPal accounts for the seller locations listed in its PayPal guide. It requires a business PayPal account for the other PayPal-supported locations. The free download still needs a connected processor.

Give away a quote and sell the tools for the job that follows

Let the free worksheet finish one poster quote. Three hours at $50 plus $40 for two rounds of revisions totals $190. At four hours, the total becomes $240. The paid pack can track a $57 deposit, a $133 balance, and an extra layout request.

Create two Digital Products and upload the appropriate files to each. Price the sample at zero. Use Unlisted when you want to test a direct link. Invisible is only for a signed-in seller. If you connect a mailing list and want downloads and email consent to be separate, set Payhip to ask buyers in every country and test what happens when consent is declined.

Use two addresses and check both sets of files

Complete the free checkout in a private window. Compare the link in the receipt with the file on the download page. Then use another email address and a product-specific 100% coupon on the paid listing. Verify that the balance is zero and the full pack arrives. A coupon checkout exercises delivery. A real payment needs a separate test.

Use real orders to decide whether the bundle is worth keeping

Bring your first readers from a newsletter, blog, or social account. Marketplace eligibility requires at least $10 in sales, a store review, and individual product approval. Self-purchases do not count. If 10 people complete free checkout after 40 visits, that measures the free step. Count paid upgrades only when you can link those purchases to people who took the sample.
