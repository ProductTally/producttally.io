Should your next Payhip product use pay-what-you-want pricing?

Compare pay-what-you-want prices for a free checklist and template, and check whether your service type is eligible.

By Evan · Updated Nov 17, 2026

Payhip lets buyers choose their amount for eligible digital and physical products. Enter 0+ to allow free downloads, or 5+ to set a minimum of five in the listed currency. Check the product type and checkout flow before trying this for an appointment-based service.

One-page filming checklist0+Whether people download and use it when free is an option
Editable template pack5+, for exampleWhether buyers pay above a minimum
Two consulting appointments per weekA fixed price and clear booking termsWhether the price covers the time and works with the booking flow

A minimum uses the listing currency. If the product is listed in euros, 5+ sets a five-euro minimum. Preview the free and paid paths separately. For a coaching appointment, verify its own fixed-price checkout and booking flow.

See what buyers see before comparing results

Walk through the free and paid flows to verify the minimum, checkout amount and receipt or download. Record visits, source and downloads. Keep the offer and placement comparable if you change pricing, and compare purchase rate and revenue per visit as well as any payments above the minimum.
