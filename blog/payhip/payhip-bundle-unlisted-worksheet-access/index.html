Payhip bundle visibility: deliver a worksheet without listing it in the store

Use Payhip's Unlisted setting to keep a worksheet out of the storefront and check how bundle buyers get it.

By Evan · Published Nov 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 27, 2026

If a Payhip bundle includes a worksheet you don't want in the public catalog, consider Unlisted. Payhip says an unlisted item is absent from the bundle sales page’s included-items list but available to eligible customers in their accounts. People with the direct product link can still access an unlisted page.

Tell buyers what they get and where to find it

For a recorded photo course with a lighting worksheet, include the worksheet in the bundle and describe it in the bundle’s public copy if it is part of the promise. Tell buyers to sign in to their customer accounts to find it. Review the storefront, bundle listing and buyer instructions in that order.

Check existing buyers before switching visibility

Make a list of previous standalone buyers and bundle customers. Payhip says changing to Invisible prevents previous customers from downloading the item. If old customers still need it, confirm the access path first. If possession of the link must never allow access to a standalone page, treat that as an additional access-control requirement.
