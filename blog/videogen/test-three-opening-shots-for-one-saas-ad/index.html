VideoGen: How do you compare three openings for the same vertical ad?

Create three 0–3 second hooks and keep the rest of the ad and the landing page identical.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Change the opening only. After 0–3 seconds, use the same demo, captions and CTA. Keep export settings, language, volume and pace consistent, and send viewers to the same page. With a fictional approval checklist as the example, make three cuts:

Cut the demo once

Make three introductions for the same approval checklist. Lead with the problem and an illustrative checklist, a verified result from a test account, or a question on a title card. Record the shared demo in VideoGen and place it after second three in all three exports. Match volume, language and framing.

Compare viewing and site actions separately

Use the same campaign objective, audience, placement and reporting period. If your report provides three-second plays, write down its definition. An illustrative 240 such plays over 1,000 impressions gives a 24% plays-to-impressions ratio. Track landing sessions and the same chosen action separately.
