VideoGen: Should you generate a software demo or record the screen?

Use generated scenes for context and record the actual controls and output for product claims.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Use generated scenes for a fictional setup or transition. For any claim about what the software can do, record the actual control and result. VideoGen Record can capture the screen and camera and send footage to its editor.

VideoGen · View product details

Decide what each shot has to prove

Show the checklist problem with an illustrative opening. For the actual action, record a test account opening the checklist, choosing a permission level and creating the link. Open that link as a second test identity and record what it can view.

  • VideoGen Record

Record before filling gaps

Close unrelated notifications and use sample data. VideoGen Record can capture the screen, camera or both and send the result to the editor. Review the recording before inserting it. Re-record at a closer scale if the permission label or result is hard to read.
