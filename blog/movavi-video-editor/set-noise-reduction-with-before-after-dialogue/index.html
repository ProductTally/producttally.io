Movavi Video Editor: How much background noise should you remove in Movavi?

Compare the original, a light pass and a stronger pass for steady fan noise and isolated clicks.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Save the original track. Compare a light and a stronger pass on the same excerpt at the same playback level. Movavi documents automatic noise removal and a manual slider. Listen for quiet consonants, word endings and the first word after a pause.

Movavi Video Editor · View product details

Prepare a steady-noise sample and an isolated knock

Use two excerpts of 10–20 seconds each. For fan noise, keep a pause and check whether speech endings remain audible. For a keystroke or knock during speech, listen for thin or unstable speech, then try editing only that portion or re-recording a key line.

Listen to the finished dialogue

Record the editor version, mode and visible strength. Lower the setting if words become harder to understand. Rejoin the dialogue and check speaker changes, loudness and peaks in the final export. These are listening checks for your own footage.
