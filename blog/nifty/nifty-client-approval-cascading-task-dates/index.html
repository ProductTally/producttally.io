Client approval moved by two days. Which Nifty task dates move with it?

Use a simple website launch to understand cascading task dates and the moment a milestone dependency makes sense.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

In Nifty, moving the date of a task can move the start and due dates of tasks that depend on it by the same number of days. Use that dependency for work that follows the approval. For a whole phase that cannot finish until the preceding phase is complete, use a milestone dependency.

Only tie dates that should move together

Say the design approval is due on December 7, development on December 10 and launch on December 14. Make development depend on approval, and launch depend on development. If the approval date moves two calendar days later, the documented cascade gives December 9, 12 and 16 in this example. An independently booked marketing interview can stay outside that chain. That frees you to move what your team controls without hiding a date that needs a separate conversation.

Check the lock before promising a new launch date

Nifty says a blocked task can still be edited, but cannot be marked complete until its prerequisite is complete. A milestone dependency goes further: tasks in the dependent milestone stay locked for completion until all prerequisite milestone tasks are complete. For one delayed approval, a task dependency makes the affected chain easy to inspect. For an approval phase with copy, pricing and design sign-off, the milestone can represent the whole decision.
