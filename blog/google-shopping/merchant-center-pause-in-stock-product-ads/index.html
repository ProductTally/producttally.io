Pause Google Shopping ads for a product that’s still in stock

Choose pause or excluded_destination by placement and duration, while keeping your product availability accurate.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

If an in-stock product is still for sale on your site, use pause=ads to stop its ads temporarily while retaining its eligibility for free listings. Google documents a limit of 14 days for pause. For longer exclusions or free listings alone, use excluded_destination.

Pause a product · Excluded destinations

Decide where the product should still appear

Say a blue mug is still shipping and customers can buy it normally. You want to take it out of paid promotion for six days. Keep its existing availability and set the pause attribute to ads for that product. Google uses ads for all supported ad placements, including Shopping ads, Display ads and local inventory ads. Choose all only if you intend to pause both ads and free listings.

  • Pause a product

Plan for exceptions before changing the feed

If the merchandiser wants a three-week exclusion, use excluded_destination for the destinations you intend to remove. Use that attribute for free listings alone as well. If the mug actually sells out and you're temporarily unable to accept orders, report out_of_stock as the real availability. Keep a note of the product ID, intended placements and day to review so the change is easy to undo for that specific item.

  • Excluded destinations

Verify the return to normal

Update the same source that supplied the attribute. Remove the temporary pause when the product should return to ads, then confirm the processed item shows the intended state and eligibility in Merchant Center. Check the actual item rather than assuming the feed update has already taken effect.
