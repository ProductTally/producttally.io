Turn a repeat customer question into a product post with Predis.ai

Start with a real photo and a question customers already ask. Make a post that shows the answer.

By Evan · Updated Nov 17, 2026

On this page

  1. Start with the question and a photo from desk level
  2. Give the generator one job
  3. Decide whether to edit, replace, or regenerate
  4. Check what the first usable post cost

The third customer this week asks whether the planner lies flat. That is your brief. Predis.ai can draft the image layout and caption together. The useful division of work is to let it propose a layout while you supply the real photo and the facts. A reader should see the answer as soon as the post opens.

Predis.ai · View product details

Start with the question and a photo from desk level

For this fictional stationery example, the planner is A5, spiral bound, and opens flat. Take a photo at desk level. Leave unconfirmed paper weight and shipping dates out. Use one image if it answers the question. Add a close-up if shoppers also ask about the binding. Predis.ai lists editable images, carousels, and captions.

  • Predis.ai social post generator

Give the generator one job

Try this brief. “Write an Instagram post that answers whether this planner lies flat in the first line. Use only A5, spiral binding, and opens flat as product facts. Use the supplied photo and end by pointing readers to the dimensions.”

Here is one way to write the caption. “Yes, it lies flat. This A5 spiral planner opens flat on a desk. Use one page for tasks and the other for meeting notes. See the listing for dimensions.”

Decide whether to edit, replace, or regenerate

Remove an invented waterproofing claim by editing the caption. Swap in the real photo if the draft changes the binding. Try video only if the reader needs to see the pages move. Before you publish, open the post on a phone and follow the product link.

Check what the first usable post cost

Record the estimate before generation. Find the actual entry under Pricing & Account → Billing Plan → Credit History. Predis.ai says generation costs vary and manual text and subtitle changes in the drag-and-drop editor use no credits. If you post this kind of answer every week, compare the time it takes to get one publishable draft with your usual process.
