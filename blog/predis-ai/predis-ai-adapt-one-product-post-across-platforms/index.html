Predis.ai: One product photo, two posts: what should change for Instagram and LinkedIn?

Use the same facts about your product to make a short demonstration and a post about a team workflow.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Use Predis.ai to turn your product photo into a short ad draft for Instagram. Write the LinkedIn post around a decision a team can make with the product. Review both against the same verified list of features and use real footage for any action viewers need to see.

Predis.ai · View product details

One board, two reasons to stop scrolling

Picture a reusable desk board with three columns and a sticky note. For Instagram, film a hand sliding the note from In progress to Needs approval. The caption can start with the reader’s next deadline: “Still waiting on tomorrow’s draft? Move it to Needs approval and write down who can unblock it.” Show the actual note moving if the clip is supposed to demonstrate how it sticks.

For LinkedIn, keep the overhead photo and start with the handoff: “At your next stand-up, ask who owns each decision in the right-hand column.” That gives a team something concrete to try. A claim about how much meeting time the board saves needs evidence of its own.

What to enter in Predis.ai

In Product Ad Video, upload your photo and enter a description. Use details you can verify, such as the number of columns and the erasable surface. The short-ad workflow also accepts special instructions. Use them to describe the shot you want, then review the draft before exporting. Draft the LinkedIn caption separately.

Before publishing, compare both pieces against the original photo and product listing. Remove accessories or discounts the draft added. Preview the video at phone size and make sure the motion is readable. On LinkedIn, check that the opening names the team’s handoff problem and leads to a relevant next step.
