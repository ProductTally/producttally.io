What can Search Console's generative AI reports tell a small site?

Separate AI impressions, pages shown and site visits. Use three examples to read the Search Console reports.

By Evan · Updated Nov 17, 2026

Use the reports to see which pages showed up in Google's generative AI features, where they appeared and how impressions changed. Google announced the Search and Discover reports in June 2026 and updated the announcement to say the insights reached websites worldwide on August 31. The views and data you see depend on your property.

Match the report to the question

Start in Performance and open Generative AI beneath Search results or Discover. Google’s current Search help says the report can be absent when the property has too few eligible impressions. An absent report provides no impression total to compare.

AI search measurement
QuestionWhere to start
Which guides appeared?Look at pages and impressions for the same period. Check that each URL points to the intended language edition.
Which market changed?Compare date and country. Note any edits or other simultaneous changes.
Did people visit the site afterwards?Compare the impression trend with independent landing-page analytics for the same dates and URLs. Keep uncertainty about traffic attribution visible.

Keep Search and Discover in separate rows. In the Search view Google documents device and search type filters. Record your view and filters before comparing weeks, and avoid adding the dedicated impressions back into the corresponding overall report.

Record facts you can verify again

Suppose a booking guide gets 30 impressions in a week. Record the URL, report type, country, dates and displayed value. Those 30 impressions alone do not reveal each query, the answer text or whether a visitor clicked. If you need to see what an answer actually said, take a separate sample with a recorded date, country and language.

Start with a few guides your site depends on. Record impressions, visits and outbound promotional clicks separately each month. If impressions grow and visits barely change, compare the same URL and window before editing.
