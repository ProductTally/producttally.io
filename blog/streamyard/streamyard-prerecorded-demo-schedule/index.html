How to schedule a prerecorded product demo in StreamYard

Plan the file, the broadcast destination and the person answering questions.

By Evan · Published Nov 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 27, 2026

A scheduled prerecorded StreamYard broadcast plays the uploaded video automatically. Use it for a finished demo when you want a specific start time. A person who needs to join on camera or change overlays during the event needs a different setup, such as running the video from a regular studio.

Prepare the file and the event together

Say you have a 20-minute product demo. Export an H.264 MP4 with the title, introduction and closing included. StreamYard supports prerecorded broadcasts up to 1080p on paid plans, with size and duration limits that depend on the plan. Choose the prerecorded option, upload the file, select your destination and confirm date, time zone, title and visibility.

  • Official documentation · streamyard.com

Give someone the audience-side job

Assign a colleague to open the watch page at the scheduled time and answer questions through the destination where supported. Keep a link to the current setup guide available. Decide in advance where unanswered questions will be collected so viewers have somewhere to go after the video ends.
