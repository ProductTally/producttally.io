A plan lost one feature. How do you make sure all four languages are updated?

Use a plan change to check guide copy, screenshots, page metadata and localized release output.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Record the change first. Suppose Starter removes its 10 GB export allowance for new subscriptions from November 15. Save the official source, effective date, market and whether existing accounts are affected. If the vendor hasn't explained grandfathering, tell existing subscribers to check their own plan.

Product pagePricing, plan comparison, FAQs and CTA notes
Related articlesCopy, share descriptions and screenshot text
Site outputLocalized URLs, internal links, structured data and dates

Use “New Starter subscriptions no longer include a 10 GB export allowance” as the English example. Keep the new-subscription scope across translations. A translation that says exports are unavailable changes the claim. If plans really differ by market, preserve the difference and link to the appropriate vendor page.

Check the rendered editions before release

Keep one record per locale with changed pages, replaced screenshots and remaining questions. Inspect the rendered HTML and the related product card. Check reciprocal hreflang where configured and use lastmod for substantial changes. After release, check the public page, then hand over the original source and review record.
