What should “the export worked” say so a reader can verify it?

Use three sample orders to document a timezone boundary, expected export and failure cases.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

State the official example, the settings it uses and its expected output. Include the plan and scope to help readers apply it to their own account.
