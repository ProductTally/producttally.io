quso.ai: How do you cut three Shorts that do not repeat the same interview quote?

Plan a question, reversal and demonstration, save the source timecodes and check the vertical export.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Give each clip a distinct question and answer. quso.ai can suggest moments from a long interview. Open each candidate in the source and check what was said before and after the cut.

Choose three moments with different jobs

  • quso.ai clip generator

The interview in this example is about turning a livestream into a course. Record the actual in and out points in your own footage.

Three cuts from one fictional interview
Fictional segmentSource timeQuestion
Problem01:05–01:39Why can't you submit an entire livestream replay as an assignment?
Reversal08:12–08:51Which seemingly faster cut took more work later?
Demonstration15:20–16:04How do you flag a section for a re-edit with one page of timecodes?

After choosing the three openings, standardize caption wording and framing across the exports.

Check the cut and the vertical export

Screen each clip on its own. Replace any pronoun that depends on an earlier question with brief context. Keep caveats that change the meaning. Leave the original in and out points in your edit notes. Preview vertically and check that captions do not cover faces or important details.

The fictional cuts run for 34, 39 and 44 seconds. YouTube currently classifies qualifying square or vertical uploads up to three minutes as Shorts. For standard channels, this applies to uploads on or after October 15, 2024. The corresponding date for Official Artist Channels is December 8, 2025. A Short over one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked globally.
