Google generative AI controls: keep Search indexing while setting limits

Choose among the Search Console control, Google-Extended, and passage-level snippet restrictions.

By Evan · Published Nov 26, 2026 · Updated Nov 26, 2026

Google updated its documentation on November 24, 2026. To stay in ordinary Google Search while opting out of AI Overviews and AI Mode, look for the Search generative AI control in Search Console. Google-Extended in robots.txt controls certain uses for training and grounding in other Google AI products.

Official source 1 · Official source 2

Decide whether the rule applies to the site or one passage

Take a public help center. If it wants to opt out of Search generative AI features across the site, use the corresponding Search Console setting. If it wants one pricing paragraph excluded from eligible snippets, apply data-nosnippet to the relevant HTML element. That choice also limits the material Google can use in ordinary search snippets.

  • Official source 2

Keep a record of what should remain available

Write down three decisions before editing: ordinary Search visibility, participation in Search AI features, and the specified uses in other Google AI products. Keep Googlebot accessible if ordinary search discovery is intended. After deploying, inspect the actual HTML or robots.txt. Protect confidential material through access controls.
