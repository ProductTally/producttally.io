Which articles do AI answers cite? Ask the same questions each month

Keep the prompt and environment constant, then record mentions, validated citations and visits separately.

By Evan · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Start with five questions your audience actually asks. Keep platform, search mode, language, region and exact prompt fixed. Use a new conversation each time and record when you ran it. Record a brand mention when a name appears. Open each source link and check whether it leads to your site and supports the answer. ChatGPT can show citations in search answers. Bing AI Performance reports citation activity across supported experiences. Keep your manual sample separate from those platform totals.

Use one row per question and answer

  • ChatGPT search citations
  • Bing AI Performance
  • OpenAI’s introduction to search citations

Keep one row per question. Record month, platform, language, region, sign-in state, prompt, search availability, mention, linked URL, reachability and whether the source supports the claim. For “How do I confirm the customer got a new invitation after rescheduling?”, a brand name alone is a mention. A working article that explains how to compare both invitations can support a citation. Record broken destinations or unsupported claims distinctly.

Suppose five preset questions receive answers, three mention your site and two link to a supporting page. Valid citations are 2/5 in this sample. Label your own test visits or exclude them from business reporting. Measure real visits and conversions in site analytics.

Repeat the same queries next month

Next month, check which sources or claims changed. Track new questions in a separate group. Log unavailable search or platform errors as missing observations and compare the questions still eligible in both periods.
