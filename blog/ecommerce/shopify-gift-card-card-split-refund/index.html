Shopify gift card and card refund: where does a partial refund go?

Review Shopify's suggested split before refunding an order paid by gift card and credit card.

By Evan · Published Nov 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 27, 2026

For a partial refund on an order paid with a Shopify gift card and another method, the default allocation goes to the gift card first, up to its refundable amount. Shopify lets you adjust each amount within the capacity of that payment method.

Official documentation · help.shopify.com 1

Follow two balances on one order

Suppose a customer paid $30 with a gift card and $70 by card. If both methods have the full amount available and you refund $45, the default example is $30 to the gift card and $15 to the card. If you want to move part of the refund to the card, review the amounts before confirming and explain the final split to the customer.

  • Official documentation · help.shopify.com 1

Tell the customer how to check

After the refund, verify the recorded split on the order and check the gift card’s updated balance. In the message, name the amount returning to each method. Avoid promising a date for the card portion when the payment record still shows it is processing.
