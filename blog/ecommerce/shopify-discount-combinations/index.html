How Shopify combines product discounts and free shipping

See how Shopify combines a product discount and free shipping, calculates the final total, and handles a cart that misses one condition.

By Evan · Published Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

If you run 20% off a product and offer free shipping on the same Black Friday order, open both discounts in Shopify and allow the other discount’s class on each. Then put the eligible item into an Online Store cart at a real delivery address. Both lines should appear when the cart meets the two offers’ conditions.

Shopify’s discount combination rules

What the buyer should see

Suppose an illustrated gift bundle costs USD 60. You offer 20% off that bundle and waive USD 8 for eligible standard shipping. The product discount takes USD 12 off the item. Free shipping then takes USD 8 off delivery. In this example, checkout should show USD 48 before tax, down from USD 68 including shipping. Write “20% off selected bundles plus free standard shipping” if those are the actual terms. The buyer can then understand why an express option still costs money.

Shopify applies product discounts first, order discounts to the revised subtotal, and shipping discounts last. If your store is eligible for product-plus-order discount combinations, adding a 10% order discount to the example takes USD 4.80 off USD 48. The new total is USD 43.20 before tax. Use that amount in your margin calculation.

Review the cart that almost qualifies

Under Discounts, open the product offer and enable combination with shipping discounts. Open the shipping offer and enable combination with product discounts. If both still sit in draft, schedule an overlapping window. Choose an eligible address, then compare that cart with one sent to a region the shipping offer excludes. If the shipping offer has a minimum, check carts just below and above it and record the subtotal the buyer actually sees.

Two rules can be individually valid and still produce a single discount. Shopify applies the best eligible discount when the available discounts cannot combine. Look at the item, shipping method and both combination settings before changing the banner.
