What to do with a Black Friday landing page after the sale ends

Keep a reusable seasonal URL useful after the offer expires, with a clear status and a current next step.

By Evan · Published Nov 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 27, 2026

When a Black Friday offer ends, keep a reusable landing page accessible if it will serve the next edition. Replace the active deal with a dated end notice and a real next step. Google’s seasonal-page guidance recommends a stable recurring URL for future events.

Official documentation · developers.google.com

Remove the promise that expired

On a reusable /black-friday/ page, put the end date near the title. Remove expired codes, checkout buttons and banners from the page and related navigation. A shopper arriving from an old email should see whether normal shopping remains available and be able to reach the current catalog without guessing. An optional reminder should say when it will be sent.

Leave a page worth revisiting

Keep the purpose of the URL consistent and retain useful sections such as how to check eligibility or shipping in the next edition. Before the next campaign, update the year, dates, copy and social preview on the same URL. Google recommends updating the page in time and requesting recrawl after changes.
