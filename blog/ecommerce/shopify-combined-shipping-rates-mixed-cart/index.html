Why does Shopify shipping change when shoppers add a second product?

Work through a two-item cart to see how Shopify combines shipping rates from different profiles at checkout.

By Evan · Published Nov 28, 2026 · Updated Nov 28, 2026

For stores still using Shopify shipping profiles, an order that spans profiles can combine their shipping rates. Rates with matching names are added together. If no names match, Shopify adds the cheapest option from each profile and displays the result as Shipping.

Combined rates

Work backward from the choices in checkout

Suppose a notebook profile offers Standard at $4 USD and Express at $9 USD. A mug profile offers Standard at $5 USD and Express at $12 USD. For the same address, under the Shopify rule for matching names, the mixed cart can show Standard at $9 and Express at $21. If the mug's rates are instead named Ground and Priority and nothing matches, the fallback is Shipping at $9. These amounts are an illustration, not a quote.

  • Combined rates

Recreate the cart before editing your rates

Open a fresh checkout with the buyer's destination. Compare the notebook alone, mug alone, and the two-item cart. In Shipping and delivery, check the assigned profiles, origins and destination zones, then compare the exact rate names. If prices still diverge, check any price-based thresholds against the cart after discounts and before tax, and check the weight and package settings for weight-based rates.

  • Shipping troubleshooting

Decide which option you want shoppers to see

Use matching service names across profiles when you intend to offer corresponding delivery choices. Treat names as part of your checkout setup and check a mixed cart after renaming a rate. Shopify is gradually moving stores to shipping options by market, so first confirm your store still uses the shipping-profile setup in the examples.
