How to record partial payments against one Flowlu invoice

Use invoice transactions to account for a deposit and a remaining balance.

By Evan · Published Nov 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 27, 2026

In Flowlu, record a payment on the original invoice and review it in Transactions. The official guide identifies partial payments and advances as uses for this tab. Invoice editing becomes unavailable after an invoice transaction is created.

Work from one invoice number

For a $1,200 design invoice, record a $400 deposit when received. Check its date, amount and reference against the payment record. The accounting example leaves $800 outstanding. When the $800 arrives, record it against the same invoice and reconcile the two entries with the original total.

  • Official documentation · www.flowlu.com

Hand off the remaining balance

Tell the next operator the invoice number, deposit reference, remaining amount and due date. Confirm billable scope and currency before posting the first transaction, since editing is then restricted. If the figures disagree, reconcile the transaction records before sending another payment request.
