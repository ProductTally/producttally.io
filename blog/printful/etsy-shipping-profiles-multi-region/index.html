How do you check a Printful shipping profile for buyers in different countries?

Compare what Etsy charges buyers and what Printful charges the seller using the same baskets and destinations.

By Evan · Updated Nov 17, 2026

For each main market, price three Etsy baskets: one item, two items in the same shipping category and a pair that might ship separately. Compare the amount Etsy collects with the amount Printful charges you.

Printful · View product details

Run both shipping calculations for each basket

Printful documents first-item and additional-item prices, with different treatment for categories that ship separately. For applicable fixed-rate Etsy baskets, Etsy uses the highest first-item rate plus the additional-item rates of the other products.

Use a real destination, item count and product configuration before publishing. These figures illustrate shipping only. They do not account for the other costs in your margin.

The profile applies to the Etsy listing. Printful says an automatically assigned profile uses the highest rate when variants have different rates. Check the most expensive and least expensive variants separately before deciding whether they belong in the same listing.

Check which profile a later sync can change

Printful pushes fixed-rate profiles to Etsy by default. Its documentation warns that synchronization can overwrite edits to a profile it manages. When you need different fees or regions, decide whether to assign a custom profile to the listing. Test both single items and bundles at the intended addresses. If the remaining gap is too large, adjust the delivery area or the listing price.
